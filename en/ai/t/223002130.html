Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Functional description

Both the current socket in the dashboard P51 and the cigar lighter/current socket P50 receive a supply from the relay R12 via a line protected by fuse F44 both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B02 . This line comes out of the control unit from pin 9 connector E and enters at pin 2 of P51 and pin 2 of P50 .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The supply for the relay R12 comes from the direct line protected by fuse F1 housed in the engine compartment junction unit B01 , whilst the supply for energizing the coil comes from the ignition switch H01 (INT/A).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

The earth is provided at pin 10 connector E of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 , at pin 3 of the cigar lighter / current socket P50 and at pin 3 of the current socket P51 .

Both the ring nut for the current socket in the dashboard P51 and the ring nut for the cigar lighter P50 are lit up (both at pin 1 of each socket) when the side lights are on via an enablement signal coming out of the body computer M01 and protected by fuse F51 of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 .
