Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the vacuum pipe from the vacuum pump by undoing the retaining collar.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the vacuum pump (2b) complete with O ring.

Recover the vacuum unit gasket from its seating.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Replace the gasket and the O-ring before refitting.

Fit the vacuum unit.

Tighten the fixing bolts to torque.

Refitting

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the brake servo vacuum pipe from the vacuum unit.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the vacuum unit (2b) complete with O-ring.

Remove the seal.
