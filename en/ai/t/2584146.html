Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Disconnect the electrical connections from the engine management control unit 1060G80 ds electric injection control unit - r r .

Insert the tool into the injector seat and lock it using the bracket.

Name

Country

1a

Dummy injector

1.870.793.000

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment.

Viat the starter motor, make the engine execute a few revolutions while holding down the accelerator and clutch pedals.

Make sure there are no leaks from the test instrument's connection.

Repeat the test on the remaining cylinders, making sure each time to reset the instrument's pen point.

Check that the cylinders' pressure measurements do not differ excessively.

Connect the electrical connections to the engine management control unit. 1060G80 ds electric injection control unit - r r
