Removing

Refitting

Raise the driver side front seat

1. Working inside the car, position the tool (1b) level with the seal (1a).

Name

Country

1b

Tool for removing front window seal

2. Move the tool along the entire perimeter of the window and remove the window complete with frame and seal.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Check that the seal is undamaged.

If it has, replace the seal.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Run an ordinary rope along the entire perimeter of the moulded seal, crossing the ends of the rope at the top of the window, leaving the ends on the inside.

The operation of fitting the window on the bodywork flange will be greatly facilitated if the rope is first impregnated in a soap and water solution.

On no account use oils or greases.

2. Fit the window (2a) in its seating, and remove the rope starting from the end (2b), checking that the rubber seal fits perfectly on the bodywork flange.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Lower the front driver's seat.
