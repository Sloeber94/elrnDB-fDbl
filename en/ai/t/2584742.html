Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift and raise it.

Op. 4450B12 FRONT WHEELS (TWO) - R.R..

Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE - R.R..

1. Undo the bolts securing the anti-roll bar to the track control arm

2. Undo bolt (2a), bolt (2b) and nut (2c) on the right and left side securing the antiroll bar struts (2d) to the front beam.

Bolt (2a) also secures the rear of the wishbone.

3. Undo the bolt securing the exhaust pipe flexible block bracket to the body.

4. Remove the anti-roll bar.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

The flexible mountings should not show signs of deterioration, like the anti-roll bar, or else the mountings and the actual anti-roll bar will have to be replaced.

Position the antiroll bar.

Tighten the bolt securing the exhaust pipe flexible mount block bracket to the body.

Op. 4450B12 FRONT WHEELS (TWO) - R.R..

Carry out final tightening of the antiroll bar fastenings under theoretical design load conditions Design load conditions on the front axle are achieved by ballasting the car (carry out this operation with the wheels fitted and the car on the ground or an appropriate lift). To do this, place 70 kg loads on the car floor in the areas in front of the right and left front seats. Then use a torque wrench to tighten the following bolts.

1. Tighten to torque bolts (1a) securing the rear of the wishbone and the antiroll bar strut to the body. tighten nut (1b) and bolt (1c) securing the antiroll bar to the beam; Tighten bolts (1d) securing the anti-roll bar to the wishbone to the specified torque.
