ATTENTION

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The engine compartment contains numerous moving parts, components at high temperatures and electrical wiring with voltage.

Closely observe the following precautions when carrying out operations in the engine compartment.

  • Turn off the engine and wait for it to cool down.
  • Do not smoke.
  • Do not use naked flames.
  • Make sure that an extinguisher is at hand in the work area.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During each refitting, replace the gaskets, oil seals, spring washers, safety plates, self-locking nut and all components which have deteriorated.
