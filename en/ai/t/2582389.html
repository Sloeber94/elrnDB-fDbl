Operation

With the engine idling, check whether the fault is present when all gears are engaged.

Eliminate any obstacles to the correct travel of the clutch pedal (positions of mats, fouling of cables, etc.).

Alignment not correct

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

After disconnecting the clutch pedal from the transmission (see Assembly 1820), check that it travels freely without tight spots or stiffness

Continue to Step 4

Play or stiffness of clutch pedal

Replace the pedal Op. 3330B26 clutch pedal - r+r

4

CONTROL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the linkage released from the clutch pedal, check that there is no hardening or sticking by sliding the cable and that the cable is free and intact

Continue to Step 5

Linkage damaged

Replace the linkage Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

5

CHECK ON PRESSURE PLATE AND BEARING (WITH GEARBOX REMOVED)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

After removing the gearbox, check that the lever is not stiff (because of dirt or insufficient lubrication) and that the clutch release bearing runs freely in its seating and shows no signs of damage

Continue to Step 6

The presence of dirt or wear caused by insufficient lubrication does not allow the clutch release bearing to slide correctly

Replace faulty or damaged components Op. 1820A24

6

CHECK ON CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visual check on the condition of the clutch assembly, which should not show breaks (pressure plate) and/or obvious damage (disc). Measurements using the gauge as per manual

Continue to Step 7

Obvious damage of clutch disc and/or breakage of pressure plate

Replace the assembly Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

7

CHECK ON CLUTCH SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • That there is no excess grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft;
  • Condition of hub and main shaft

Continue to Step 8

Main shaft damage, hub worn, excessive grease on bushes

Replace the hub and/or main shaft Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + ror OVERHAULING GEARBOXES Assembly 2110B

All OK

Problems met

Operation

3

CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF AIR IN CLUTCH HYDRAULIC SYSTEM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Adjust the bleed screw on the operating cylinder to check that the system is free of air

Continue to Step 4

Presence of air in the hydraulic system

Bleed the air Op. 0010T67 hydraulic clutch system bleed air

Continue to Step 4

4

CHECK ON CLUTCH OPERATING CYLINDER (ON CAR)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Release the cylinder from the clutch control lever, keeping it connected to the hydraulic system.

Press the pedal to induce the thrust pressure and check the pushrod travel.

Using a punch, push the pushrod back in, checking that it travels freely

Continue to Step 5

Malfunction of clutch operating cylinder

Remove the clutch operating cylinder Op. 1820B30 OPERATING CYLINDER/CLUTCH HYDRAULIC ACTUATOR - R.R.

Continue to Step 5

5

CHECK CLUTCH OPERATING CYLINDER (WITH GEARBOX REMOVED)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the part on the bench, apply pressure (with compressed air) and check the pushrod travel (the test mirrors what was done on the car)

The purpose of this test is to ensure that the fault is in the cylinder and not the pipes or pump

Continue to Step 6

Malfunction of clutch operating cylinder

Replace the clutch operating cylinder Op. 1820B30 OPERATING CYLINDER/CLUTCH HYDRAULIC ACTUATOR - R.R.

6

CHECK ON CLUTCH PEDAL TRAVEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the system disconnected, check that when the pedal is pressed and released, the travel is correct

Continue to Step 7

Malfunction of hydraulic pump

Replace the pump Op. 1820B20 (Pump) - MASTER CYLINDER R.R.

Hose from clutch reservoir to pump worn or deformed

Replace the pipe Op. 1820B40 FLEXIBLE PIPE from clutch reservoir to (pump) - master cylinder R.R.

Supply hose to clutch operating cylinder worn or deformed

Replace the pipe Op. 1820B48 FLEXIBLE supply PIPE to operating cylinder -R.R.
