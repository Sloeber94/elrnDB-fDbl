Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a device which makes it possible to directly adjust the headlamp beam, according to the load, from the driver's seat. The headlamps are thus always aligned quickly and correctly.

The adjustment device consists of a motor mounted on each of the two headlamps which tilts them as appropriate, lowering the light beam if the car is heavily laden, and raising it if the load decreases.

A specific knob on the facia next to the dashboard is used to select the four possible positions.

The position selected is shown in a display in the instrument panel.

The device is only enabled when the dipped headlamps are switched on.

The headlamp adjustment circuit is protected by the same fuses as the dipped beam headlamps circuit, located in the junction unit under the dashboard.
