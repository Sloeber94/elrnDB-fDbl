Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is also available, on request, prepared for the installation of a cellular telephone.

This preparation is configured as follows:

  • dual function aerial (radio and cell phone);
  • 'hands-free' speaker, located in the passenger side door (it is the same speaker as the radio with two connections: one for the radio and one for the cellular telephone 'hands-free' function);
  • hands-free microphone located inside the front courtesy light.

The connector for the cellular telephone has two supplies; one direct from the battery and one only with the ignition switched ON.

The two lines are protected by fuses located in the junction unit under the dashboard and the junction unit in the engine compartment.
