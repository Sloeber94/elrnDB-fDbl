1088E09 radiator cooling fan - r r with air conditioner

Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical supply connector (1a) for the fan and the electrical connections (1b) for the fan resistor

Release the electrical wiring from the retaining bands.

Name

Connector

1a

Engine electric fan motor

See N11 ENGINE ELECTRIC FAN MOTOR ( RADIATOR )

Name

Connector

1b

Engine fan adjustment resistor

See O010 ENGINE FAN ADJUSTMENT RESISTOR - 1

Open the band and release the lower engine coolant pipe.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the fan to the radiator.

3. Undo the nut (3a) fixing the fan to the upper crossmember and remove the engine fan (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.4 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the fan and secure it using the fixing nut and bolts.

Connect the electrical connection.

Secure the fan wiring to the bands.

Position the engine coolant pipe and tighten the securing band.

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.
