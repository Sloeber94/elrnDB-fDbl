1. Fit the flywheel lock.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the clutch mechanism and the plate (2b).

The bolts are pre-treated with sealant and therefore they should be replaced each time they are refitted.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005
