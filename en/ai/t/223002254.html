Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

When reverse gear is engaged the positive signal (INT/A) for switching on the reversing lights moves via the junction unit uunder the dashboard B02 and supplies the parking sensors control unit M84 at pin 1 connector A via pin 8 connector D.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

The control unit M084 is earthed at pin 16 of connector A.

The parking sensor speaker P44 receives signals from pins 2 and 10 of connector A of the parking control unit M84; pins 9 and 6 of the same connector are bridged.

The control unit M84 provides a power supply and reference earth to the four parking sensors K97 from pins 6 and 1 of connector B respectively.

The four front parking sensors K97 sends signals to connector B of the control unit M84:

- to pin 5 the left external rear sensor K97E;

- to pin 3 the left internal rear sensor K97F;

- to pin 2 the right internal rear sensor K97G;

- to pin 4 the right external rear sensor K97H;
