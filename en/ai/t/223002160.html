Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: 7 seater and MY 2005

For safety reasons, it is not permitted to travel with the seats in the second row tilted and the seats in the third row fitted in normal service position: in particular, if the middle seat in the second row is tilted with the third row seat in normal position, a buzzer sounds to warn that the travelling position is incorrect on start-up (key ON).

It is absolutely forbidden to travel with the seats in the second row tilted and passengers seated in the third row (as indicated on the seat plate).

The seat position indicator device circuit is protected by a special fuse located in the under-dashboard control unit.
