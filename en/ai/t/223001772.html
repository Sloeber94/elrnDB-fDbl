Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The luggage compartment is lit (lighting present only on Doblò version), via a courtesy light on the left hand side. The light comes on when the side-hinged or up and over door (according to the version) is opened by means of the boot opening switch.

the boot light circuit is supplied directly from the battery.
