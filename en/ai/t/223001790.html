Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Description

All the instruments (dials and gauges) are lit up with the ignition switched ON.

All the instrument panel LEDs are always lit up at the same brightness.

The LEDs for lighting the instrument scales are activated when the side lights on message is received from the body computer.
