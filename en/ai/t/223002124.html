Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Doblò trim level and MY 2005

The electric windows for the front doors are operated through the switches on the driver's door (both windows) and the passenger side (passenger side only).

Both windows are operatd only with key ON.

Both windows are operatd only with key ON.

Operation is allowed for a further two minutes after switching the key from ON to OFF, only with the doors closed.

The electric window circuit is protected by two fuses, one for the driver side and one for the passenger side, and controlled by a specific relay located in the under-dashboard control unit.
