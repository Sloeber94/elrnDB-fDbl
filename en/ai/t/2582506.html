Whilst driving round a bend (right or left) there is a low frequency noise from the area of the gearbox.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

FIRST CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Whilst driving, steer to the right or the left and check the onset of the noise.

Continue to Step 3

Noise does not vary with the steering angle

Continue to Step 1

Noise varies with the steering angle

Continue to Step 2

1

DIFFERENTIAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the differential: In particular, check the bearings

Continue to Step 3

Bearings damaged

Replace the bearings Op. 2110D26 differential inner case bearings - replace with differential inner case removed

Internal gears worn or damaged

Overhaul or replace the differential housing Op. 2110D10 differential inner case - r + r with manual gearbox removed - includes gearbox dismantle and rebuild

2

JOINTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the transmission joints

Continue to Step 3

Wheel side joint damaged

Replace the joint Op. 2710A54 l/h or r/h wheel end constant velocity joint (one) - r + r with drive shaft removed - including boot replacement

Differential side joint damaged

Replace the joint Op. 2710A52 differential end sliding joint (one) l/h or r/h - r + r with drive shaft removed - includes boot replacement

3
