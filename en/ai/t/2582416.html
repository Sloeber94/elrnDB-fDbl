Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the rear fog lamp switch is operated with the dipped headlamps on (or the fog lights on) the rear fog lamp does not work. If the dipped beam headlamps do not work either I05 DIPPED HEADLAMPS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the EXAMINER diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the Body Computer.

Continue to Step 2

Rear fog lamps open circuit error or incorrect bulb or short circuit to earth or short circuit to + V

Check the condition of the bulbs

Continue to Step 2

Battery

Restore the correct connections

Continue to Step 3

2

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

Bulbs damaged

Replace bulbs Op. 5540A50 tail-light bulb (one), lh or rh - replace

Bulb holder assembly faulty

Renew or replace the rear light cluster Op. 5540A44 l/h or r/h tail light (one) - r+r

3

CHECK REAR FOG LAMP ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between rear fog lamp switch and Body Computer
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs
  • bulb earth
  • rear fog lamp switch earth : See E2031

Continue to Step 4

Connections broken

Restore connections See E2031

Faulty earth

Renew the earth : See E2031

4

CHECK REAR FOG LAMP SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check rear fog lamp switch operation See E2031

Continue to Step 5

Rear fog lamp switch faulty

Replace the control panel Op. 5505A24 w/l and service switch mount - r+r

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
