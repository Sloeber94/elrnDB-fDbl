Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Doblò trim level

Description

The vehicle is available with a radio system fitted on request.

The system consists of the following components:

  • radio (CD / cassette player);
  • radio supply cables;
  • two front speakers located in the doors and two in the dashboard;
  • two rear speakers;
  • leads connecting the radio to the speakers;
  • aerial;

aerial coaxial cable.

The radio system is equipped with a protocol for communicating with the electronic unit which manages the electrical equipment (body computer).

When the radio is supplied, the body computer reads the radio code and establishes whether or not to activate it.

The radio circuit is supplied with the key turned to the ON position, or directly from the battery (which allows tuning settings, security codes, etc. to be stored).

The two supply lines are protected by three fuses, two of which are housed in the junction unit under the dashboard and the third in the junction unit in the engine compartment.
