1088B32 engine cooling radiator support beam - r r

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE - R.R.

Attach the radiator to the body shell using cord or metal wire

Raise the ramp.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and (1b) fixing the crossmember to the bodyshell and to the plastic side shields.

2. Und the bolts fixing the crossmember to the front bumper.

Release the rubber mountings retaining the radiator and the windscreen washer fluid reservoir from the crossmember.

3. Remove the engine radiator support beam.

If the vehicle is equipped with a power assisted steering pump, the rigid pipes for the power assisted steering fluid cooling coil must be released from the fastenings on the crossmember.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the crossmember, fastening the rubber mountings for the radiator and the windscreen washer fluid reservoir.

Tighten the fixing bolts to the bodyshell, the front bumper and the wheel arch liners.

Tighten the bolts fixing the crossmember to the bodyshell to torque.

Removing

Assemble the supercharging air/air heat exchanger and the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember and secure it using the bolts.

Place the engine cooling radiator supporting crossmember, complete with supercharging air/air heat exchanger and secure it using the bolts.

Take care to ensure that the windscreen washer fluid reservoir lug engages in the housing in the radiator supporting crossmember.

Connect the heat exchanger air outlet hose and tighten the band.
