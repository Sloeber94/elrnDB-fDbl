Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The compressor activation circuit is controlled by relay R5, contained in engine bay junction unit B01. This is supplied directly by a line from the battery that enters at pin 1 of control unit connector A of the control unit and is protected by fuse F19.

The coil of relay R5 is supplied by a signal from relay R9 that in turn receives a direct supply from the battery at pin 1 connector A.

Relays R9 and R5 are excited by an earth signal from engine management unit M10 respectively from pins 80 and 79 of connector A.

In this way, control unit M10 controls the activation of air conditioner compressor L20 via relay R5.

Engine compartment control unit M10 receives signals from 4 level pressure switch K10 to turn on the radiator fan:

- operation at first speed from pin 27;

- operation at second speed from pin 26;

- air conditioner activation from pin 28.
