Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The interior lights do not work when the relevant controls are operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of individual bulbs and bulb holders (which often contain the light switch)

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs damaged

Change bulbs

Bulb holder assembly faulty

Repair or replace the relevant bulb holder

2

CHECK ON INTERIOR LIGHTING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • service fuse
  • light power supply
  • light earth
  • connection between light and switch (if outside light) and lights
  • connection between switch (if outside light) and earth See E2510

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
