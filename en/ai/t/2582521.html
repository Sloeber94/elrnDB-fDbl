WIND NOISE AREA LOCATION

Operation

Test the vehicle until the speed is reached when the problem occurs to locate the area from which the wind noise is coming (if necessary, use bodywork adhesive tape)

Continue to Step 2

The wind noise is coming from the side doors

Verify:

  • door alignment
  • door window alignment
  • door housing surrounding seal
  • glass surrounding seal

The wind noise is coming from the hinged opening side window

Verify:

  • glass alignment
  • surrounding seal

The wind noise is coming from the rear pillar cover

Verify:

  • cover fixing
  • cover alignment

The noise is coming from the rear doors

Verify:

  • door alignment
  • door housing surrounding seal

The wind noise is coming from the wheel arches

Verify:

  • fastening/alignment of additional wheel arch liner
  • compartment seal
