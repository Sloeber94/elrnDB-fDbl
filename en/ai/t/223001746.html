Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods; only with equipment: Doblò trim level

Description

The lighting of the luggage compartment (on the Doblò only) is through the special courtesy light on the left of the luggage compartment when the door is opened (the type of which depends on the version) thanks to the boot opening switch.

The luggage compartment light circuit is supplied directly by the battery.
