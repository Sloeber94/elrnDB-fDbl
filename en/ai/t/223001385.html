Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Functional description

The car earth points are subdivided according to the wiring to which they are connected, as follows:

  • negative battery wiring C01, C40
  • front wiring C10, C12
  • radiator wiring A01F, A01G
  • dashboard wiring C15A, C50
  • rear wiring C15C, C30, C53
  • engine wiring A01H, C11, C40A, C40B, C60, C62
