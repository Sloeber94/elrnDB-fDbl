Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the belt fastened and the vehicle stationary, when making gentle movements (for example, picking up an object) the seat belt locks easily and/or remains locked

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

REEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the reel is fitted correctly, checking that the reference pin is correctly positioned in the bodyshell housing

Continue to Step 2

Fitting irregular

Loosen the fixing bolt and position the reference lug correctly Retighten the bolt to the recommended torque

2

CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF FOREIGN BODIES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no foreign bodies inside the system

Continue to Step 3

Presence of foreign bodies

Remove foreign bodies

3

BELT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is no interference or distortion acused by accidents

Continue to Step 4

There is interference slowing down the sliding of the belts

Eliminate the interference, if necessary, replace the damaged components

Presence of distortions on the front mounting rod

Replace the front rod Op. 7065A14 ANCHORAGE ROD for , (one), front seat belt left or right - R.R.

Presence of distortion on the rear mounting rod

Replace the rear rod Op. 7065B14 Rear seat belt ANCHORAGE ROD , (one), left or right - R.R.

Presence of distortion on the front reel/pretensioner

Replace the front belt Op. 7065A10 , (One), front SEAT BELT left or right - R.R.

Presence of distortion on the rear reel

Replace the rear belt Op. 7065B10 l/h or r/h rear safety belt (one) - r+r

4

PRETENSIONER OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the pretensioner has not been activated as the result of an accident. If this is the case, there would be white powder on the spring casing, the reel would be locked and/or the belt deteriorated

Continue to Step 5

Evidence of pretensioner activated

Replace the belt Op. 7065A10 , (One), front SEAT BELT left or right - R.R.

5

REEL RELEASE SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the reel release, rewinding the belt for a brief section and gently unwinding it

Continue to Step 6

Front reel release system faulty

Replace the front belt Op. 7065A10 , (One), front SEAT BELT left or right - R.R.

Rear reel release system faulty

Replace the rear belt Op. 7065B10 l/h or r/h rear safety belt (one) - r+r

6

CORRECT COMPONENTS FITTING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the right/left components are fitted on the correct side of the vehicle

Continue to Step 7

Fitting not correct

Restore the correct fitting and, if necessary, replace the components

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
