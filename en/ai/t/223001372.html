Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Functional description

The car earth points are subdivided according to the wiring to which they are connected, as follows:

  • negative battery wiring C01, C10, C40 ;
  • front wiring A01F, C10, C60 ;
  • dashboard wiring C15 A, C15 B, C15 C ;
  • rear wiring C30 ;
  • Air Bag / Pretensioner wiring C50, C53, C54 ;
  • ABS wiring C12 ;
