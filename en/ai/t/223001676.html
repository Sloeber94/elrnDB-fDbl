Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Description

The front interior light includes two courtesy lights with two control switches; the rear interior light, on the other hand, has only one courtesy light.

The control switches can be used to select the following operating modes for the front courtesy light:

  • with the left switch in the centre position: the lights go on/off when the doors are opened/closed;
  • with the left switched pushed towards the left: the lights are constantly off;
  • with the left switched pushed towards the right: the lights are constantly on;

With the courtesy light off, the right switch can be used to switch on the left or the right light, individually, according to the position of the actual switch.
