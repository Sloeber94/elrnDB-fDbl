MECHANICAL CONTROL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the linkage disconnected from the pedal, check that there is no hardening or sticking and that the cable is free and intact by sliding it

Continue to Step 5

Linkage damaged

Replace the linkage Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

5

CHECK ON PRESSURE PLATE AND BEARING (WITH GEARBOX REMOVED)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

After removing the gearbox, check that the lever is not stiff (because of dirt or insufficient lubrication) and that the clutch release bearing runs freely in its seating and shows no signs of damage

Continue to Step 6

The presence of dirt or wear caused by insufficient lubrication does not allow the clutch release bearing to slide correctly

Replace faulty or damaged components Op. 1820A24

CHECK ON CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visual check on the condition of the clutch assembly, which should not show breaks (pressure plate) and/or obvious damage (disc). Measurements using the gauge as per manual Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

Continue to Step 7

Obvious damage of clutch disc and/or breakage of pressure plate

Replace the assembly Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

7

CHECK ON CLUTCH SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • That there is no excess grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft
  • Condition of hub and main shaft

Continue to Step 8

Main shaft damage, hub worn, excessive grease on bushes

Replace the hub and/or main shaft Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + rOVERHAULING GEARBOXES Assembly 2110B

CHECK ON CLUTCH OPERATING CYLINDER (ON CAR)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Release the cylinder from the clutch control lever, keeping it connected to the hydraulic system.

Press the pedal to induce the thrust pressure and check the pushrod travel.

Using a punch, push the pushrod back in, checking that it travels freely

Continue to Step 8

Malfunction of clutch operating cylinder

Remove the clutch operating cylinder Op. 1820B30 OPERATING CYLINDER/CLUTCH HYDRAULIC ACTUATOR - R.R.

Continue to Step 6

6

CHECK CLUTCH OPERATING CYLINDER (WITH GEARBOX REMOVED)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the part on the bench, apply pressure (with compressed air) and check the pushrod travel (the test mirrors what was done on the car)

The purpose of this test is to ensure that the fault is in the cylinder and not the pipes or pump

Continue to Step 7

Malfunction of clutch operating cylinder

Replace the clutch operating cylinder Op. 1820B30 OPERATING CYLINDER/CLUTCH HYDRAULIC ACTUATOR - R.R.
