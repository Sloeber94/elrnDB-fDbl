Removing

Refitting

Carry out

Op. 7020C12 SLIDING SIDE DOOR END OF TRAVEL STOP LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R..

1. Withdraw nuts (1a) on the link adjustment sleeve and withdraw the end from the pin (1b).

2. Unscrew the lower sliding guide bolts.

3. Remove the device holding the sliding door open.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: 7 seater

Refitting

Removing

Position the device holding the sliding door open in its seat and secure to the lower sliding device with bolts.

Tighten the nuts on the adjustment sleeve and insert the end of the link in the pin.

Carry out

Op. 7020C12 SLIDING SIDE DOOR END OF TRAVEL STOP LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R..
