Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The front and rear door lock motors are operated simultaneously by using:

- the key;

- the front door switches (knobs);

- the remote control.

Lock operation: all four side doors lock simultaneously when one of the two front doors is locked with a key or remote control when all the doors are properly closed.

Lock release operation: the door locks of the four side doors release when one of the two front doors is unlocked using the key or remote control.

This control logic is controlled by the body computer node
