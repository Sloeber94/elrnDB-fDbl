Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Check the tyre tread is not irregularly worn.

Some types of tyre are fitted with wear gauges; these tyres should be replaced as soon as these gauges are visible on the tread.

Do not swap tyres across.

Check the tyre inflation pressure is correct. Also check the spare wheel.

The tyre pressures must be checked when they are cold.

Op. 4450B12 FRONT WHEELS (TWO) - R.R.

then visually inspect the condition of the front disc brake pads and ensure they are not worn.

Op. 3320B34 REAR BRAKE DRUMS - R.R.

then visually check the wear of the brake linings.

Check that the protective boots for the driveshafts, power steering and steering joints do not show signs of abrasion or breaks.

Check that the braking and fuel system pipes/connectors are not leaking.
