Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Functional description

When reverse gear is engaged, the positive signal for switching on the rear lights (

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

) passes through the short circuit coupling D97A . The positive supply for the parking sensor control unit M84 reaches pin 1 of connector A from pin 10 of D97A . The negative supply for the control unit M84 reaches pin 8 of connector A from the dashboard earth C15C .

The parking sensor speaker P44 receives signals from pins 2 and 10 of connector A for the parking control unit M84 ; pins 9 and 6 of the same connector, on the other hand, are bridged.

The four parking sensors K97 are connected to sensor B of the control unit M84, respectively:

  • to pins 5, 9, 12 sensor K97A ;
  • to pins 7, 3, 10 sensor K97B ;
  • to pins 1, 2, 6 sensor K97C ;
  • to pins 8, 4, 11 sensor K97D .
