Op. 4450B13 REAR WHEELS (TWO) - R.R..

Op. 3320B34 REAR BRAKE DRUMS - R.R..

Raise the lift.

1. Remove plug (1a) and disconnect handbrake cable (1b) from the fitting on the shoes, working from the front side of the shoe carrier plate.

2. Pull the cables out from the shoe carrier plate

3. Undo the bolts securing the cable retaining bolts.

4. Release the handbrake cable brackets from the rear leaf springs.

5. Remove the handbrake cables.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
