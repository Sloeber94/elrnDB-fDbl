Results of the tests

1

STEERING COLUMN FIXING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixing of the steering column: it should not be slack

Continue to Step 2

Steering column slack

Restore the correct fastening

2

END FLOAT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the end float of the steering column: it should not be excessive

Continue to Step 3

Clearance excessive

Restore the correct clearance, fixing the pin. If necessary, replace the steering Op. 4120A10

3

SPIDER CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is no spider clearance for the steering column rod

Continue to Step 4

Spider clearance

Replace the rod Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

4

COLLAPSIBLE ROD CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is no clearance for the collapsible rod

Continue to Step 5

Rod clearance

Replace the rod Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

5

RACK CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rack clearance, turning the steering to the end of travel position: the clearance should not be too large

Continue to Step 6

Excessive clearance at the rack bushes

Replace the steering box Op. 4120A10

6

STEERING ROD CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clearance of the steering rods: it should not be too large

Continue to Step 7

Clearance excessive

Replace the steering Op. 4120A10

7

STEERING BOX FIXING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixing of the steering box: it should not be slack

Continue to Step 8

Fixing slack

Tighten the pins to torque
