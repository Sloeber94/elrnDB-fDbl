Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The horns are operated by pressing one of the two areas at the sides of the steering wheel.

The horn circuit is protected by a specific fuse and controlled by a relay located in the engine bay junction unit.

For safety reasons, the horn can be operated at any time (even with key off).
