Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

A cigar lighter is housed to the right of the ashtray. This is surrounded by an lighted ring with the side lights on.

Press the cigar lighter knob to operate the lighter: after a few seconds it will emerge automatically, ready for use.

An extra power point to the left of the ashtray may be used to connected 12V instruments or devices.

The cigar ligher and power point circuit is protected by a specific fuse and controlled by an ignition-operated cut-out relay located in the under-dashboard junction unit.

The power points are supplied on with the key ON.
