Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the centre of the steering wheel cover is pressed, the horns do not work or they sound out of tune

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

HORNS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the horns.

Continue to Step 2

Horns faulty.

Repair the horns. Op. 5550B30 l/h or r/h horn (one) - r+r

Horns working out of tune

Replace the horns Op. 5550B30 l/h or r/h horn (one) - r+r

2

HORNS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Horns fuse
  • Horns relay
  • The horns supply and earth
  • Continuity and insulation of electrical circuit between dashboard control unit and horns
  • See E3010

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E3010

3

CLOCK SPRING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Connection between switches on steering wheel and clock spring.
  • Connection of the steering column switch unit with the clock spring
  • Operation of the clock spring
  • See E7010

Continue to Step 4

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E7030

Clock spring faulty

Replace the clock spring Op. 5580C12

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
