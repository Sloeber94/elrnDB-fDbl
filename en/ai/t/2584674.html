Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Remove a front wheel, proceed as in the example

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R..

Using a syringe, drain the brake fluid from the reservoir.

1. Remove the spring for the connector between the flexible pipe and the rigid pipe.

2. Use tool (2a) to undo fitting (2b) and use a normal fixed wrench to undo connector (2c), then remove hose (2d).

Plug the pipe to prevent fluid from coming out.
