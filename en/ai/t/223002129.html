Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Description

There is a cigar lighter on the right of the ashtray. It has a ring nut which is lit up when the side lights are on.

To operate the cigar lighter, simply press the knob: after a few seconds it pops out automatically, ready for use.

On the left of the ashtray, on the other hand, there is an additional current socket which can be used for connecting instruments or equipment operating at 12 V.

The circuit for the cigar lighter and the current socket is protected by a special fuse and controlled by an ignition-operated cut-out relay located in the junction unit under the dashboard.

The sockets are only supplied when the ignition is switched ON.
