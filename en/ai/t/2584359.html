Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The manual procedure and the teser procedure for re-initalising the electronic ignition/injection control unit is shown below.

When the car is first started, carry out the following operations whenever the throttle body or engine management unit is replaced.

During this procedure, from the moment when the instrument panel is supplied with power until the end of learning, do not press the accelerator, brake or clutch pedals.

Procedure using tester

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position.

Connect the tool to the tester input and place the knob in position 3.

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment.

Turn the ignition key to ON position

Wait for the test to be run on the throttle body, when the tester will confirm that

Turn the ignition key to the OFF position

Using the Examiner, check that the self-learning of idle speed has finished correctly ( LOWER THROTTLE BED SELFLEARNING- completed)

If the self-learning procedure is not correct, turn the ignition off (STOP position) and repeat the above procecure. If the incorrectness continues, check that the diagnostic cable connection is secure and that the instrument is working.

Manual procedure

Turn the ignition key to ON position

Wait 60 seconds, the time required for the control unit to check the throttle body's internal spring and the butterfly's maximum/minimum angles.

Turn the ignition key to the OFF position

Wait 10 seconds, the time required for the control unit to save the learnt values in EPROM.

After carrying out the initialisation procedure, the power supply may be disconnected from the engine control unit (battery disconnection) without losing data.
