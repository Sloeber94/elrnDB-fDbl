Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is equipped with reversing lights located in the tail-light clusters.

These are operated whenever reverse is engaged via a switch on the outside of the gear housing.

The circuit is supplied with the key on MAR and protected by a fuse located in the underfacia junction unit.

The reversing light activation signal is managed by the Body Computer, which forwards this information:

- to the Parking Sensor Control Unit;

See E3090 PARKING SENSOR

- and to the Radio Navigation System;

See E4090 NAVIGATOR

- while also inhibiting the seat belt buzzer.

See E7030 AIRBAG
