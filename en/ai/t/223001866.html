Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The steering column switch unit windscreen wiper remote control switch H05 makes it possible to select the various windscreen wash/wipe operating modes.

The power supply for the steering column switch unit H05 reaches pin 11 of connector A via the output at pin 10 connector A of the junction unit under the dashboard B02.

Inside the junction unit B02 this line comes from relay F12 via a line protected by fuse F43.

The power supply at the relay arrives from the line protected by fuse F1 housed in the junction unit B01 from pin A connector C of the junction unit under the dashboard B02.

The operation of the relay F12 is determined by the ignition controlled supply line (INT/A) entering the junction unit under the dashboard B02 at pin 1 connector E.

According to the function selected, stalk unit H05 (connector A) sends the following control signals to the electronic device inside wiper motor N15:

- from pin 9 for operation at first speed;

- from pin 8 for operation at second speed;

- from pin 6 for zeroing.

The steering column switch unit H05 also supplies the wind/rearscreen washer pump N22 sending a supply signal and an earth signal from pins 12 and 10 connector A.

The steering column switch unit H05 is earthed at pin 7 connector A and at pin 5 connector C.

The windscreen wiper motor N15 is earthed at pin 5.
