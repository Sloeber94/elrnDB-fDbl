N01 CIGAR LIGHTER/CURRENT SOCKET NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the cigar lighter is pressed or a consumer connected to the special connection, the cigar lighter / current socket does not work.

The power sockets all have a heat protection device: if devices with high energy absorption are connected, this protection cuts off the supply.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING CIGAR LIGHTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the cigar lighter/current socket: in particular, make sure that the thermal protection device inside the socket has not been affected due to overloading

Continue to Step 2

Thermal protection affected

Replace the cigar lighter/current socket Op. 7065C72

2

CHECKING CIGAR LIGHTER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Cigar lighter fuse (in dashboard control unit)
  • Relay (in dashboard control unit)
  • Cigar lighter power supply and earth
  • Continuity and insulation of cigar lighter electrical circuit : See E3070

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections See E3070
