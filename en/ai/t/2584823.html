Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

1. Undo the upper bolts fixing the condenser to the engine radiator.

2. Release the pins of the radiator-condenser assembly from the retainers (2a) to gain access to the lower fixing bolts (2b), then undo them.

Be careful with the cooling system pipes and the radiator, securing them suitably to avoid damage during removal of the condenser.

3. Remove the condenser from the top.
