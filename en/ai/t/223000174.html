Refitting

Before removing and refitting the Air Bag deceleration sensor the following preliminary operations must be carried out.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Lift the bonnet

- Disconnect the negative and positive battery terminals and insulate them.

- Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.
