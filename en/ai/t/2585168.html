Removing

Refitting

1. Use a slide control lever (1a) to move guide (1b) in order to unscrew retaining bolts (1c).

2. Remove retaining clip (2a) and separate guide (2b) from the slide control lever (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Carry out the same operation for the remaining guide and recover the slide control lever.

The front seat left side guide is secured using three bolts while the right guide is secured using two bolts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
