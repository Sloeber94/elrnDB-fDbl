Refitting

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the steering column bottom trim (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the FIAT CODE aerial from the ignition switch.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connector (1a) for the switch and, acting on the tabs (1b), remove the contact block (1c).

Avoid the drive and the spacer coming out of the contact block housing to prevent refitting them incorrectly.
