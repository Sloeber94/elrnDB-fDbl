Refitting

2. Remove the seal.

Refitting

Removing

Before fitting the new filter element, remove any impurities from the filter casing (through suction) that could fall into the "clean air" part of the circuit.

Position a new air cleaner filter element in its housing.

Place the gasket in its seat.

Place the air filter cover back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Refitting

2. Remove the filter element from the casing.

Removing
