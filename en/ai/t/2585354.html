Basic operation

The test must be carried out at an ambient temperature of between 20°Χ ανδ 30°Χ.

Close all the doors and windows.

Set the gearbox to neutral.

Start the engine and run it at a speed of 1250-1300 rpm.

Open the central air vents and position a thermometer probe inside.

Set the temperature selector to the coldest setting.

Set the fan speed selector to 2nd speed.

Set the distribution selector to the 'front' position.

Activate the air conditioner.

Activate recirculation

Check that within 2-4 minutes the thermometer's temperature reading stabilizes at 8°-11°C.
