Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is equipped with a very bright rear fog lamp located in the rear of the left light cluster (right light cluster for right hand drive versions) and can be used in all poor visibility conditions.

The rear fog lamps is activated using the special button in the switch unit in the middle of the dashboard under the centre air vents.

The enablement for switching on the rear fog lamp depends on the dipped headlamps or the fog lights being in use.

A warning light in the instrument panel signals that the rear fog lamps are on.

The rear fog lamps check function is available on the version with a Comfort instrument panel.

Any fault can be recognized by the vehicle branch (left or right side), in which case the Body Computer transmits the failure status via the CAN to the instrument panel which lights up the special "exterior lights failure" warning light and simultaneously shows the information in the display.
