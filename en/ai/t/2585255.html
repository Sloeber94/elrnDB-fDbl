Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before the repair in question, the vehicle should be in the following condition: tail-gate and wing removed, check carried out for deformations in connected elements and adjacent body parts straightened before proceeding.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Check if any of the connected parts are deformed by checking body alignment dimensions. Use appropriate measurement tools (reference rigs, templates or gauges).

Straighten the body if necessary.

1. Reduce the spot welds on the edges of the rear pillar.

2. Using a pneumatic saw, cut the upper part of the rear pillar following the cutting lines shown in the diagram.

3. Using a pneumatic saw, cut the lower part of the rear pillar following the cutting lines shown in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the rear pillar trim.

2. Use suitable pliers to remove the cut edges from the body.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

2. Apply electro-galvanized paint to the body edges affected by the welding.
