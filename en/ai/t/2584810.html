1. Release the transmission cable from the fixing (1a) and disconnect the flexible transmission cable (1b).

Removing

1. Connect the air mixer transmission cable (1a) and tighten the screw (1b).

2. Connect the air distribution transmission cable (2a) and tighten the bolt (2b).

3. Connect the flexible transmission cable (3a) and secure it to the attachment (1b).
