1040A37 fuel filler pipe housing seal - r r

Path
Procedures > 10 ENGINE > 1040 FUEL TANK AND PIPES > 1040A FUEL TANK AND COMPONENTS > 1040A37 fuel filler pipe housing seal - r r
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fDbl/en/#/2584224

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Partly raise the lift.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe fitting to the bodyshell.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel vapour filter mounting bracket.

3. Gently lower the fuel filler pipe (3a) and remove the gasket (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit a new gasket.

Tighten bolts securing the fuel filler fitting mounting bracket.

Secure the fuel filler fitting to the bodyshell with the securing bolts.

Lower the lift.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v; all production periods

Fit a new gasket.

Tighten bolts securing the fuel filler fitting mounting bracket.

Fasten the fuel filler to the bodyshell using the fixing bolts.

Lower the ramp.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the rigid section of the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

2. Lower the fuel filler pipe (2a) as necessary, then remove the gasket (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit the fuel filler pipe housing seal.

Place the fuel filler pipe back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Remove the car from the ramps.
