Refitting

Working from inside the vehicle, proceed as described below.

Op. 7040A60 UNDERDASHBOARD TRIM - R.R.

Op. 2125C16 GEAR HAND LEVER SUPPORT - R.R.

Move the two front seats forwards and pull the handbrake lever until it is in the end of travel position.

1. Remove the coin holder (1a) and the protective cover (1b) behind the handbrake lever.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2b) and move the tunnel cover (2b) back.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Unscrew the retaining bolts

2. Remove the two shields for the heater unti air vent.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Unscrew the retaining bolts

2. Remove the gear lever mounting bracket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
