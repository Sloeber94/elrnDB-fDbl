K53 I.E. FAILURE WARNING LIGHT OFF / (DO NOT CARRY OUT INITIAL TEST)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The engine management control unit warning light does not come on. The CODE warning light comes on and goes out.

The CODE light going out normally means that the dialogue between the two control units has had a positive outcome. The failure of the engine management control unit warning light to come on is due to a fault in the warning light operating circuit. The failure to come on is caused by supply system problems.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
