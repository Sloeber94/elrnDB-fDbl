Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Cargo trim level and MY 2005

Operation of the radio system P20 is controlled by the body computer by lines leaving pins 5 and 17 connector E of junction unit B02 and entering pins 1 and 3 connector A of radio P20.

The radio is supplied directly (pin 7 connector A) from the battery via the line of fuse F1 contained in engine compartment junction unit B01 and protected by fuse F39 of under-dashboard junction unit B02.

Radio P20 receives an ignition-operated power supply (INT) (pin 4 connector a ) via the line of fuse F49 of under-dashboard junction unit B02.

Radio P20 receives at pin 6 of connector A a power supply with side lights on for interior lighting via a line controlled by body computer M01 and protected by fuse F51 of junction unit B02.

Cables carrying signals for the speakers leave connector B:

- right front side on dashboard P38 from pins 3 and 4 via short-circuiting junction D97 B;

- left front side on dashboard P37 from pins 5 and 6 via short-circuiting junction D97 D;

Pin 5 connector A is connected to aerial amplifier P25 while pin 8 connector A is connected to earth.

Speaker P37 assumes a dual coil configuration when the mobile phone wiring is installed.

See E3580 PREPARATION FOR CELLULAR TELEPHONE

The body computer is supplied via pin 11 connector E (INT) and pin A connector C (direct power supply) of junction unit B02. Fuse F1 in junction unit B01 protects the direct power supply.

Pins 8 and 9 of connector D of the body computer are earthed.
