Carry out this check with the vehicle on a flat surface, several minutes after the engine has been switched off.

When the engine is warm, do not remove the reservoir cap; danger of burns.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Check that the coolant level is between the MIN and MAX marks on the expansion tank.

1. If necessary, top up the level by unscrewing the cap and adding engine coolant
