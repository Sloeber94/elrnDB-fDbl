Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Cargo trim level and MY 2005

The front door windows are operated by switches located in the middle of the dashboard.

Operation is manual: the glass goes up/down only with the key pressed. Both windows are only operated only with the key ON.

On some versions if the driver's electric window button is pressed for more than half a second the automatic operation is activated: the window stops when it reaches the end of travel position or if the button is pressed again.

The operation is allowed for a further two minutes after having turned the ignition key from ON to OFF, but only with the doors closed.

The electric windows circuit is protected by two special fuses, one for the driver's side and one for the passenger side and is controlled by a special relay, located in the junction unit under the dashboard.
