1. Release the transmission cable from its fixing (1a) and disconnect the flexible transmission cable (1b).

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and disconnect the air distribution transmission cable (2b).

3. Undo the bolt (3a) and disconnect the air mixing transmission cable (3b).

4. Remove the complete heater controls mounting.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Connect the air mixer transmission cable (1a) and undo the bolt (1b).

2. Connect the air distribution transmission cable (2a) and tighten the bolt (2b).

3. Connect the flexible transmission cable (3a) and secure it to the attachment (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
