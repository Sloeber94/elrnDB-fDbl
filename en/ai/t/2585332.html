Disconnect the electrical connections from all the fuel injectors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or Examiner Plus)

Display the 'measure diesel pressure' and 'engine rpm' parameters on the parameters screen of the Control Unit Tests.

Turn the ignition key to the start (AVV) position for 5 seconds and check that the fuel pressure is over 300 bar with engine speed of about 200 rpm.
