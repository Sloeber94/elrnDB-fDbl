(*) Capacity for the methane canisters in standard conditions: 15 °C - 1.013 bar - density 0.680 kg/m^3

It should be pointed out that when using a fuel like methane the range is very variable because in addition to the vehicle maintenance and driving conditions it also depends on the temperature of the ga in the canister. Methane heats up during filling and cools down during driving undergoing changes in pressure that reduce the quantity that can be used.
