Removing

Refitting

Op. 7020E34 REAR DOOR FLAP INTERIOR PANEL - R+R

1. Disconnect the rigid control for opening/closing the door via the outer handle, lock side.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the lock (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Connect the rigid control for opening/closing the door via the handle.

Loosely tighten the lock bolts.

Op. 7020E34 REAR DOOR FLAP INTERIOR PANEL - R+R

Check that the lock and its striker are correctly aligned.

If it is not, adjust the lock, using the slotted holes on it.

After completing the adjustment, tighten the lock bolts.
