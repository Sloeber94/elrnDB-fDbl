Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Doblò trim level and MY 2005

The front interior light includes two courtesy lights with two control switches. The rear interior light consists of a single light.

The control switches may be used to select the following front courtesy light operating modes:

- with left switch in central position, the lights go on/off when the doors are opened/closed;

- with the left courtesy light pressed to the left, the lights are always off;

- with the left courtesy light pressed to the rightt, the lights are always on;

With the courtesy light off, the right switch can be used to turn on the right or left lights individually according to switch position.

Two different timing strategies are used to making it easier to get into and out of the vehicle.

The front courtesy light comes on when getting into the vehicle as follows:

- for 15 seconds when the front doors are opened;

- for seven seconds when one door is closed (within three minutes).

In each case, the timing is stopped by turning the ignition key ON.

The courtesy light timing strategy when leaving the vehicle (i.e. when the ignition key is removed) is as follows:

- within two minutes of turning off the engine for three minutes;

- for seven seconds when one door is closed (within three minutes).

The timing ends automatically when the doors are locked.

The operating strategy for the rear courtesy light depends on the key position as follows:

- light comes on when the tailgate or sliding side doors are opened if the switch is in central position;

- light comes on irrespective of whether tail-gate or sliding side doors are opened if the switch is set to the right;

- the bulb is always off with the switch to the left.
