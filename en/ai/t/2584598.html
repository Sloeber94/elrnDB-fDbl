Removing

Refitting

Working from inside the vehicle, proceed as described below.

1. Lift up the cover, releasing it from the lower plastic pins.

2. Lift up the cover, cut the fastening band (2a) and remove the actual cover (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the cover on the lever and, using a new band, secure it to the upper part of the actual lever.

Secure the plastic surround for the gear lever cover to the lower dashboard cover.
