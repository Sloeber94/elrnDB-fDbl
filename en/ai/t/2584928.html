Removing

Refitting

Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R

1. Release the positive (1a) and negative (1b) battery leads from the retaining springs on the drip tray and place them to the side.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the battery drip tray (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the drip tray and tighten the fixing bolts.

Secure the positive and negative battery leads to the retaining springs.
