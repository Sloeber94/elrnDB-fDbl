1040B42 filter/carburettor line - r r

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the pipe from the quick release fitting (1a) on the fuel filter and from quick release fitting (1b) on the injection pump.

2. Remove the pipe from the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the rapid attachment of the fuel return pipe, fuel return manifold pipe side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the pipe and connect the ends to the fuel filter and injection pump.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the delivery pipe from the fuel filter to pressure pump, pressure pump side.

2. Disconnect the rapid attachment of the supply pipe from the fuel filter to the pressure pump, fuel filter side.

3. Remove the supply pipe from fuel filter to pressure pump (3a) after releasing it from the fixings (3b) on the air chamber.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit in its seating the supply pipe from fuel filter to pressure pump, and secure it to the air chamber with the fixings.

Connect the rapid attachment of the supply pipe from fuel filter to pressure pump, fuel filter side.

Connect the delivery pipe from fuel filter to pressure pump, pressure pump side, and tighten the band.

Connect the rapid attachment of the fuel return pipe, fuel return manifold pipe side.
