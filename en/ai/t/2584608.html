2710A30 intermediate drive shaft - r + r with r/h drive shaft removed

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts fixing the intermediate driveshaft to the support.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

1. Remove the intermediate driveshaft.

2. Remove the dust excluder.

Collect the transmission fluid which comes out in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the intermediate driveshaft, complete with dust excluder, back in its housing.

Take care not to damage the differential side oil seal whilst fitting the intermediate driveshaft.

1. Tighten the bolt fixing the intermediate driveshaft to the support to the recommended torque.

Refitting

1. Undo bolts (1a) securing the intermediate drive shaft to mount (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Withdraw and emove the intermediate driveshaft with mount.

Collect the fluid emerging from the differential in a suitable container.

2. Remove the dust cover from the intermediate drive shaft.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods
