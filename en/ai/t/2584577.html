Op. 5530B52 BATTERY SUPPORT/DRIP TRAY - R.R.

Using a syringe, drain the brake/clutch fluid from the reservoir.

1. Raise the retaining clip (1a) and disconnect the fluid pipe (1b) from the slave cylinder.

2. Raise retaining clip (2b) and disconnect the pipe from master cylinder (2b), then remove the pipe from the engine bay.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

Refitting

Removing

Connect the pipe to the master cylinder and operating cylinder and fix it with its clips.

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.
