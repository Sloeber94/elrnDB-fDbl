Removing

Fit the new rigid pipe and secure to the bulkhead between engine bay and passenger compartment using brackets.

When positioning the rigid pipe, take care not to damage or bend it.

Screw the rigid pipe fitting onto the hose and tighten using a wrench. Then do up the brake pipe retaining clip.
