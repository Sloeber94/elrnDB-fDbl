The climate control is working properly, but there is excessive noise coming from the distributor duct assembly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING ELECTRIC FAN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check climate control system fan

In particular, ascertain:

  • No foreign bodies inside fan casing;
  • there are no vibrations at the various speeds (causing operation with the fan switched off, but the electrical connection engaged)

Continue to Step 2

Fan dirty

Clean the fan casing and all the ducts

Electric motor noisy, fan unbalanced

Replace the fan Op. 5040C30 fan motor - r+r

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments), carry out (in climate control control unit mode) active diagnosis of the fan at the different speeds

Continue to Step 3

Electric fan operation irregular

Replace the fan Op. 5040C30 fan motor - r+r

3

CHECKING DISTRIBUTOR DUCT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for the presence of foreign bodies and damaged parts inside the distributor duct assembly

Continue to Step 4

Presence of foreign bodies or damaged parts inside the duct distributor assembly

Overhaul the duct distributor assembly Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

4

CHECKING THE AIR DUCTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the air ducts inside the dashboard and the vents are intact and that there are no foreign bodies which could cause noise

Continue to Step 5

Ducts obstructed or damaged

Repair the ducts and vents

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
