1036H57 hydraulic tappets all r r

Removing

Refitting

Position the car on the ramp.

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Name

Connector

-

Battery

A1

Drain the air conditioner coolant circuit 0010T84 coolant for air conditioning system dehydrate and recharge .

Carry out 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Carry out 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Carry out 1060G90 diesel injection wiring harness - r r .

Carry out 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Carry out 4450B04 right front wheel r r .

Carry out .

Carry out 1016B10 single cylinder head extension - change gasket .

1. Remove the valve rocker arms.

2. Remove the hydraulic tappets.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Lubricate with engine oil and refit the hydraulic tappets in their seatings.

Position the hydraulic tappets with the oil passage hole facing towards the outside of the bottom cylinder head: this position puts this hole in communication with the oil channel present in the bottom cylinder head.

Place the valve rockers in their housings.

Carry out 1016B10 single cylinder head extension - change gasket .

Carry out .

Carry out 4450B04 right front wheel r r .

Carry out 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

Carry out 1060G90 diesel injection wiring harness - r r .

Carry out 5530B52 Battery DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R. .

Carry out 5530B10 battery - r+r .

Fill the air conditioner coolant circuit 0010T84 coolant for air conditioning system dehydrate and recharge .

Connect the battery negative terminal.
