Refitting

Check if any of the connected parts are deformed by checking body alignment dimensions. Use appropriate measurement tools (reference rigs, templates or gauges).

Straighten the body if necessary.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Use a pneumatic saw to cut along the cutting line shown in the upper part of the corner trim.

2. Use a chamfering machine and drill to reduce the weld spots on the edges of the trim and along the areas of contact with the underlying frame.

3. Disengage the corner trim from the underlying sealant applied along the window compartment area.

4. Remove the rear corner trim

5. Use a hammer and chisel to remove the reinforcement shown

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Straighten the edges of the panel.

Use a grinding wheel to remove the weld spot residues.

Apply electro-galvanizing paint to the parts which have been ground.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
