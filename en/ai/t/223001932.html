Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Description

The two external rearview mirrors are adjusted by the control located near the actual mirrors. The two mirrors are adjusted by a single button pressed in the four directions: a switch selects the right or left mirror.

The button, which can be pressed in four directions, controls two electric motors located in each of the two mirrors: one motor rotates the mirror on a horizontal axis, the other on a vertical axis.

The adjustment circuit for the rear view mirrors is protected by a fuse located in the junction unit which protects other supplies controlled by the ignition.

The adjustment is only possible with the ignition switched ON.
