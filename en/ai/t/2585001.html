1. Prise the window top guide slightly from its seating.

2. Open the safety stop (2a) and remove the window from the mounting (2b) on the guide.

3. Carefully remove the window.

Removing

1. Fit the window (1a) in its seating, fitting the hole (1b) on the pin (1c) of the mounting (1d) on the guide.

2. Close the safety stop.
