With the ignition on, when the lever on the steering column switch is raised or lowered or when the appropriate button is pressed, the direction indicators/hazard warning lights work but flash irregularly

If one of the bulbs is not working, the intermittent flashing of the others is affected:

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

CHECKING BULBS

All OK

Operation

Check that the bulbs are of the correct rating

Continue to Step 2

Front bulb rating incorrect

Replace the bulb Op. 5550C18 bulb of one front turn signal, l/h or r/h - r+r

Rear bulb rating incorrect

Replace the bulb Op. 5550C26 l/h or r/h side turn signal bulb - r+r

Side repeater rating incorrect

Replace the side repeater Op. 5550C22 l/h or r/h side flasher (one) - r+r
