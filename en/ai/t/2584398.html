After removing and changing the fuel filter, start up the engine and keep it idling for a few minutes to bleed out any air from the system. The presence of air in the pump may cause the injection warning light on the instrument panel to come on because the internal sensors detect that the pump is not working properly. If this happens, use a Tester to remove the error stored by the control unit.
