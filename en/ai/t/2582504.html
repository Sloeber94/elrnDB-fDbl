S22 NOISE' GEARBOX / IN NEUTRAL

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle stationary, the gear lever in neutral and the engine running, there is a high frequency, low intensity noise in the area of the gearbox.

The fault is often accompanied by an oil leak from the gearbox: X10 ENGINE COOLANT LEAKS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

GEARBOX BEARING CONDITIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Overhaul the gearbox Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings

In particular, check the gearbox bearings: they should not show signs of wear

Continue to Step 3

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • Clutch plate wear;
  • Clutch plate fouling (oil or grease)
  • Clutch plate not distorted;
  • No damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring;
  • No damage to the flexible coupling springs

Continue to Step 4

Clutch disc or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r
