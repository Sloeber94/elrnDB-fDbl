Refitting

Removing

If the air bag is replaced because it has been triggered or expired, proceed as follows:Detach the removable part of the adhesive label from the new module. Place a copy of the label in a specific file containing the vehicle details (model, registration data, chassis number) and the serial number of the old module. This file should be kept for future checks.Apply the detachable part of the original label to the bottom of the seat, where there are already similar labels for the seat, squab cover and Air Bag wiring. However, it should first be perforated to indicate the month and 14 years after the year when the module is fitted (e.g. 2017 will correspond to 2003).

Position the air bag module in its seat and connect the electrical connectors.
