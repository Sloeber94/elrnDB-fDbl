PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Press the clutch pedal and check whether there is excessive resistance

Since the symptom is 'subjective', make sure that there really is hardening compared with the previous condition which was considered 'normal', or compared with similar cars

Continue to Step 1

-

1

CLUTCH PEDAL POSITION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check the alignment of the clutch pedal in relation to the brake pedal

Continue to Step 2

Alignment not correct

Adjust the clutch cable linkage Op. 1820A14 clutch pedal - adjust travel

2

CHECK CLUTCH LEVER TRAVEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, inside the engine compartment, that when the clutch pedal is pressed, the clutch control lever on the gearbox performs the correct travel

Continue to Step 5

Incomplete clutch lever travel on gearbox

Continue to Step 3

3

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

After disconnecting the clutch pedal from the transmission (see Assembly 1820), check that it travels freely without tight spots or stiffness

Continue to Step 4

Play or stiffness of clutch pedal

Replace the pedal Op. 3330B26 clutch pedal - r+r

4

MECHANICAL CONTROL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the linkage disconnected from the clutch pedal, check that there is no hardening or sticking and that the cable is free and intact by sliding the cable

Continue to Step 5

Linkage damaged

Replace the linkage Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

5

CHECK ON PRESSURE PLATE AND BEARING (WITH GEARBOX REMOVED)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

After removing the gearbox, check that the lever is not stiff (because of dirt or insufficient lubrication) and that the clutch release bearing runs freely in its seating and shows no signs of damage

Continue to Step 5

The presence of dirt or wear caused by insufficient lubrication does not allow the clutch release bearing to slide correctly

Replace faulty or damaged components Op. 1820A24

6

CHECK ON CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visual check on the condition of the clutch assembly, which should not show breaks (pressure plate) and/or obvious damage (disc). Measurements using the gauge as per manual Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

Continue to Step 7

Obvious damage of clutch disc and/or breakage of pressure plate

Replace the assembly Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

7

CHECK ON CLUTCH SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • That there is no excess grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft
  • Condition of hub and main shaft

Continue to Step 8

Main shaft damage, hub worn, excessive grease on bushes

Replace the hub and/or main shaft Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + ror OVERHAULING GEARBOXES Assembly 2110B

8

FLYWHEEL CONDITION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the flywheel. In particular, check:

  • Condition of surface;
  • Wear;
  • Chips;
  • Signs of overheating

Continue to Step 9

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed
