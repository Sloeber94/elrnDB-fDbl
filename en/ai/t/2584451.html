Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the pipe from rapid attachment (1a) on the injection pump and from band (1b) on the fuel filter.

2. Disconnect the injector recirculation pipe

3. Remove the pipe from the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the pipe and connect the ends to the fuel filter and injection pump.

Connect the injector recirculation pipe.
