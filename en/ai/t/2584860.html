Removing

Refitting

1. Lift up the wiper arm.

2. Press on the lug (2a) of the support (2b) and withdraw the blade (2c) from the end of the arm.

Different types of blades are fitted on the vehicle; follow the instructions on the packaging.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the blade is not damaged, that the pins do not have excessive play and that the blade profile is intact.

Fit the support in the end of the arm and engage the retainer.

Lower the wiper arm.

Check that the window wiper is working properly.
