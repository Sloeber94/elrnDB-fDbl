Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Description

The vehicle climate control system involves a duct/distributor unit with a heater, controlled via three knobs in the dashboard:

  • the left knob mechanically controls the mixture flap between hot and cold air;
  • the right knob, mechanically operated via a cable, controls the opening of the flaps which regulate the distribution of air flows;
  • the centre knob electrically controls the ventilation of the air at four different speeds; two versions are available: one with three speeds and one with four speeds;

The operation of the fan at different speeds is achieved through a resistive divider, protected by a special thermal fuse to prevent overheating.

There is also a sliding knob on the control panel, located under the knobs, which mechanically regulates the 'recirculation' function via a cable.

The control panel is lit up when the side lights are on.

The fan circuit is protected by a specific fuse, in the junction unit in the dashboard, controlled by the passenger compartment air fan relay, located in the duct/distributor unit.

The fan only operates when the ignition is switched ON.
