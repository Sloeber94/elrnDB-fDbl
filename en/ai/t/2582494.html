Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During starting, the 'Air Bag failure' warning light does not come on (it should come on for 4 seconds to indicate that the initial control unit test is being carried out and then go out)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket.

Check the connection betwen air bag control unit and instrument.

Continue to Step 2

Connection not correct

Restore connection

2

CHECKING INSTRUMENT PANEL OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check instrument panel is working properly See Test 5580CD Air Bag warning light check

Continue to Step 3

Instrument panel LED open circuit

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
