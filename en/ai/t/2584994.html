It is not possible to order a single lock barrel or to order barrels using a single part code. When ordering barrels, observe the special procedure set out in operation 5580 - Special Accessories.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Using a suitable tool, remove the anti break-in device.

2. Remove the retaining clip.

3. Insert the key and remove the lock barrel from its seating.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the retaining clip in its seating.

Insert the lock barrel in its seating until it clicks into place.

Insert the anti break-in device.
