Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Functional description

The signal for activating the reversing light leaves the switch I20 , located on the gearbox, whose supply is controlled by the ignition (INT/A) via the connection between the ignition switch H01 and pin 1 connector E of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 . The line is protected by fuse F31 housed in the same junction unit ( B02 ) and passes through the engine compartment junction unit B01 entering at pin 11 connector B and exiting at pin 9 connector B.

When reverse gear is engaged, the switch closes and the reversing lights in the right rear lights F31 and the left rear lights F30 are supplied via pin 8 of connector D of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 .

At the same time, this supply is sent to the body computer M01 for the connected logic functions.

The body computer is supplied via pin 11 connector E of the junction unit B02 from the ignition switch H01 (INT), or via a line protected by fuses F53 and F1 housed in junction units B02 and B01 , respectively, and, lastly directly from the battery via the protective fuse F1 in the engine compartment junction unit B01 .

Pins 8 and 9 connector D of the body computer are connected to earth.
