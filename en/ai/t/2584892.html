Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

If necessary, check the continuity.

Connect a sensor to the wiring and apply vaseline to the edge of the rubber cable duct to facilitate the fitting in the housing.

Pass the wiring into the vehicle and then fit the rubber cable duct in place.

Connect the windscreen washer fluid supply pipes to the windscreen and rearscreen to the electric pump.
