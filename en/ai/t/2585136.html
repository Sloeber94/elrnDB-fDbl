Refitting

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the lower steering column cover (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the gear lever boot.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Open the glove compartment.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the trim under the dashboard (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the trim under the dashboard and secure it using the bolts.

Position the gear lever boot.

Fit and secure the lower steering column cover using the bolts.

Connect the negative battery terminal.
