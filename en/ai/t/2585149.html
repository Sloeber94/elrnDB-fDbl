Refitting

1. Remove the knob for the seat belt height adjuster.

2. Open the plastic protection (2a), undo the fixing bolt (2b) and place the upper rod (2c) for the front seat belt at the side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Doblò trim level
