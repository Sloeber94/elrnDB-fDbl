Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is fitted with an extra bright rear fog lamp, located in the left rear light cluster which can be used in all poor visibility conditions.

The rear fog lamps are activated by a button in the switch unit on the central console beneath the central air diffusers.

The rear fog lamps are enabled when the dipped beams are on, or when the fog lights are on.

A warning light in the instrument panel signals that the rear fog lamps have been switched on.
