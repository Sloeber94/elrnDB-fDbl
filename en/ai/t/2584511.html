Removing

Place the flexible pipe from the engine oil heat exchanger to the rigid pipe for the water/exhaust gas heat exchanger for the E.G.R. system back in its housing and secure it.

Connect the flexible pipe from the engine oil heat exchanger to the rigid pipe for the water/exhaust gas heat exchanger for the E.G.R. system, rigid pipe side and tighten the band.

Connect the flexible pipe from the engine oil heat exchanger to the rigid pipe for the water/exhaust gas heat exchanger for the E.G.R. system, heat exchanger side and tighten the band.

Remove the car from the ramp.
