Removing

Check that the flexible cables for opening/closing the door from the outer and inner handle are not damaged.

Fit, in their seatings, the flexible control cables for opening/closing the door.

Fit the door lock gearmotor complete with flexible control cables, and secure it with the bolts to the specified torque.
