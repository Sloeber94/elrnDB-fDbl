Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Basic operation

Connect the tester (Examiner or other instruments) and carry out active diagnosis 'Zeroing phonic wheel initialisation'

Bring the engine up to temperature (temperature greater than 80°Χ) from minimum level. Accelerate to 5000 rpm (constant) for about 2-3 seconds and release. Carry out this operation for three consecutive times.
