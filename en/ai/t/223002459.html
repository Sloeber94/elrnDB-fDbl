Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The radio phone connection D75 receives a direct power supply (pin 9) from the battery via the line for fuse F39 of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 and protected by fuse F1 of the engine compartment junction unit B01.

It receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) at pin 7 via the line for fuse F49 of the junction unit under the dashboard B02.

The power supply with the side lights on (POS) reaches pin 8 at the radio phone connection D79 via the line protected by fuse F51 of the junction unit under the dashboard B02.

Pin 10 of connection D75 is earthed, whilst the cables leave pins 5 and 6 with signals for the right front speaker in the dashboard P38 at connector A (left front speaker P37 at connector A for right hand drive versions).

The radio phone connection D75 exchanges information via pins 4, 3 and 1 with the radio P20 (pins 7, 8 and 9 connector C).

A connection for the mobile phone aerial is located near connector D75.
