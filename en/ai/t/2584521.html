Removing

Refitting

Place the vehicle on a lift.

Drain the high pressure circuit as described in the specific operation 1042A02 C.N.G. - FUEL SYSTEM Safety check .

1. Disconnect the connector on the recharging pipe from the one-way valve.

2. Unscrew the one-way valve (2a) by means of the special notches, and remove it complete with sealing ring (2b).

3. Recover the inner spring and piston.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Cargo trim level and BIPOWER

Refitting

Removing

Position the piston with spring on the solenoid.

Tighten the one-way valve complete with new sealing ring on the solenoid.

Connect the connector of the recharging pipe on the one-way valve.

Manually set the CNG cut-off solenoids to the open position and check that the system is working.

Connect the Examiner diagnostic instrument in the 'Active Diagnoses' environment, and select and run 'Gas solenoids relay' to allow the solenoids to open and to restore the CNG system operating pressure; read the pressure value given by the sensor (located on the pressure reduction unit in the engine compartment) which should be greater than or equal to 11 bar, otherwise it will not be possible to switch from petrol to methane.

Remove the vehicle from the lift.
