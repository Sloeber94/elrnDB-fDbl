On setting off, when the various gears are engaged and the clutch released, or at the start of deceleration, there is a noise (jolt)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

GEARBOX SUPPORTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gearbox support fastenings: they should not be slack

Continue to Step 2

Nuts slack

Restore the correct tightening of the slack nuts

2

CHECKING THE CROWNWHEEL AND PINION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the crown wheel and pinion reduction In particular: it should not be worn or damaged; there should not be excessive clearance the crownwheel fixings should not be slack

Continue to Step 3

Clearance excessive

Restore the clearance or, if necessary, replace the reduction gear Op. 2110D28

3

DIFFERENTIAL HOUSING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the wear of the satellite carrier bearing or the housing in the differential housing

Continue to Step 4

Casing worn

Overhaul or replace the differential housing Op. 2110D10 differential inner case - r + r with manual gearbox removed - includes gearbox dismantle and rebuild

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
