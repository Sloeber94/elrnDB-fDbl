Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the top and bottom centring pins (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the top and bottom centring pins and loosely tighten the fixing bolts.

Check that the sliding side door closes properly, and that the centring pins on the door are properly aligned with the references on the central pillar.

If not, adjust the position of the centring pins, using the slots for securing the bolts.

Tighten the top and bottom centring pins bolts to torque.
