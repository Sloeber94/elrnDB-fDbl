Refitting

Removing

Fit the two float valves complete with pipes.

Fit the complete fuel filler.

Connect the fuel filler pipe and the reflux pipe, securing them to the tank using new bands.

Connect the pipes connecting the vapour separator to the float valves to the rapid connectors.

1. Disconnect the rear section of the single supply pipe from the pump.

Fit the fuel pump and tighten the ring nut.

Replace the gasket under the fuel pump and take care when refitting to check that the gasket is correctly positioned ensuring the seal.

Tighten the ring nut to torque using the special tool.
