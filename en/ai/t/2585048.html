7020C15 Upper sliding GUIDE for one sliding side door, left or right - R.R.

Removing

Refitting

Carry out

Op. 7020C40 SLIDING SIDE DOOR UPPER GUIDE TRIM (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R..

Position a prop beneath the sliding door, opened to half way across the opening.

1. Loosen bolts (1a) for bracket (1b) covering the groove on the upper guide.

2. Undo bolts (2a) and remove upper slide guide (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: 7 seater

Refitting

Removing

Position the upper slide guide in its seat and insert into the upper sliding device.

Tighten the upper guide bolts, position the groove cover bracket on the guide and tighten the bolt.

Remove the prop beneath the door.

Carry out

Op. 7020C40 SLIDING SIDE DOOR UPPER GUIDE TRIM (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R..
