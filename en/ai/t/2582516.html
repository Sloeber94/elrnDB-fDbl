When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from one of the front doors

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING DASHBOARD/DOOR PANEL MATCHING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door panel and facia fit together properly

Continue to Step 2

Dashboard fixing faulty

Repair the facia fastening Refit the felt pads if necessary

2

CHECKING DOOR FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fastening

  • Of the hinges (upper and lower)
  • Of the lock

Continue to Step 3

Hinge fixing faulty

If necessary, lubricate the hinge pin. Repair the fastening Op. 7005A24 front side door r r

Lock fixing faulty

Refit the lock correctly Op. 7005N21 front side door lock motor-lock assembley-r r

Striker faulty

Refix the striker correctly Op. 7005B14 front side door catch striker r r

3

CHECKING DOOR PANEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the door panel Op. 7005E10 front side door inner panel - r r for replacementand check for foreign bodies inside the door

Continue to Step 4

Presence of foreign bodies

Remove foreign bodies

4

CHECKING INTERNAL DOOR FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fastening

  • Of the window opening;
  • Of moving window glass guides;
  • Of the element fixing the moving window;
  • Of the door opening/closing linkage

Continue to Step 5

Window fastening faulty

Repair window fastening Op. 7005M21 front side door electrically operated window - r r

Front guide fastening faults

Refit the guides correctly Op. 7005D22 front side door descending window glass guide (one) - r r with door panel (and support frame) removed

Fastening element faulty

Replace the fastening element Op. 7005D15 front side door descending window glass r r

Linkage fixing faulty

Renew the linkage fastening springs

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
