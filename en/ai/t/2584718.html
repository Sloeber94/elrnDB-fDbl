Refitting

Place the vehicle on a lift.

Open the bonnet lid and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the lower steering column shaft to the pinion on the power assisted steering box; place the lower steering column shaft to one side.

Before removing the steering column from the power assisted steering box, ensure the front wheels are straight.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

2. Release the pipes from the retaining band.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
