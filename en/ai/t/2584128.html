0010T64 brake and or clutch fluid check level and top up as necessary

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Check the brake fluid in the reservoir is to the maximum level.Otherwise top up with the requird fluid. If the brake fluid level is topped up, remove the reservoir cap and top up to the maximum level.

ORDINARY PREPARATION

The symbol on the container identifies synthetic type brake/clutch fluids, distinguishing them from mineral type fluids. The use of mineral type fluids irreparably damages the special rubber seals in the hydraulic brake circuit.

Take great care to prevent the brake fluid, which is extremely corrosive, from coming into contact with the paintwork. If this does happen, was immediately with water.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

Check that the brake fluid in the reservoir is at the maximum level; if this is not the case, top up using the recommended fluid.

When topping up the brake fluid, remove the reservoir cap and top up until the maximum level is reached.
