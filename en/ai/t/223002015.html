Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Description

The lock motors on all the doors are activated simultaneously upon a command:

  • from the ignition;
  • from the front door switches (knobs);
  • the remote control.

Locking operation: when the key is used on one of the two front doors or the remote control is operated when the doors are firmly shut, all four doors are locked simultaneously.

Unlocking operation: when the key is used on one of the two front doors, or the remote control is operated, all four doors are unlocked.

This system is controlled by the body computer.
