Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Cargo trim level

Description

The vehicle is available with a radio system fitted on request.

The system consists of the following components:

  • radio (CD / cassette player);
  • radio supply cables;
  • two front speakers in the dashboard;
  • aerial;
  • aerial coaxial cable.

The radio circuit receives a direct supply from the battery (which allows tuning settings, security codes, etc. to be stored).

The above line is protected by two fuses in the engine compartment junction unit and in the junction unit under the dashboard.
