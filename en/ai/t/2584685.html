Refitting

Place the car on a lift.

Disconnect the battery negative terminal lead.

Remove a front wheel, proceed as in the example

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R..

1. Undo the bolt securing the front wheel rpm sensor.

2. Working in the engine bay, disconnect the rpm sensor electrical connection near the front suspension dome.
