Removing

Refitting

- Lift the bonnet

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

1. Unscrew bolts (1a) securing the grille to front bumper (1b) and remove the bumper from the vehicle.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the grille on the front bumper is undamaged.

- Position the grille on the front bumper in its saet and secure with bolts.
