Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Functional description

The activation of the main beam headlamps is controlled by an earth signal coming from the steering column switch unit H05 by rotating a ring nut and it reaches the body computer M01 at pin 33 connector F.

To switch the main beam headlamps on, the body computer M01 energizes a relay R2 , housed in the engine compartment junction unit B01 , from pin 9 connector C. Another energizing signal, coming from the ignition switch H01 , reaches the relay R2 at the same time and enters pin 30 connector B of the engine compartment junction unit B01 .

The latter receives the supply directly from pin 1 connector A and sends it to the right and left headlamps at the outlets at pins 22 and 25 of connector B, respectively.

Each line is protected by a fuse in the engine compartment junction unit B01: F15 for the left headlamp, F14 for the right headlamp.

Body computer M01 also controls the activation of the light on the instrument panel E50 .

The body computer can be supplied via pin 11 connector E (supply controlled by the ignition) and pin A connector C (direct supply) of the junction unit B02 . There is a protective fuse F1 for the direct supply located in the junction unit B01 .

These lines also supply the instrument panel E50 via two lines protected by fuses F37 and F53 .

Pins 8 and 9 connector D of the body computer are connected to earth.
