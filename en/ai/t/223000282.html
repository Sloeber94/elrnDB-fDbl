Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Functional description

A line (50) leaves the ignition switch H01 from pin 3 connector A coming directly from the battery. With the ignition key in the (AVV) position, the starter motor A20 solenoid winding is supplied at pin C.

Pin A of the starter motor receives the voltage coming directly from the battery A01 connector B.

The direct current produced by the alternator A10 (pin B+) is sent to recharge the battery A01 , passing through the motor A20 pin B. When the alternator does not turn and therefore does not recharge the battery, it sends an earth signal from pin D+ to the body computer M01 pin 19 of connector C. This controls the lighting up of the 'battery recharging' warning light in the instrument panel E50 from pins 5 and 6 of connector D (CAN line).

When the engine has been started up, this signal becomes 12 V and the warning light therefore goes out.

The body computer and the instrument panel communicate with one another via pins 5 and 6 connector D of the computer and pins 9 and 8 connector A of the panel.

The body computer can be supplied via pin 11 connector E (supply controlled by the ignition) and pin A connector C (direct supply) of the junction unit B02 . There is a protective fuse F1 for the direct supply located in the junction unit B01 .

These lines also supply the instrument panel E50 via two lines protected by fuses F37 and F53 of junction unit B02 and enter the panel at pins 1 and 3 connector A.

Pins 8 and 9 connector D of the body computer are connected to earth.
