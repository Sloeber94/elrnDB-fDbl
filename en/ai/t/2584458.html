Removing

Refitting

1. Fit the two bolts for stopping the rotation of the injection pump pulley into the holes made on the pulley.

2. Unscrew the pulley securing bolts.

Remove the bolts fitted previously for locking the pulley.

3. Remove the injection pump pulley.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the injection pump pulley.

Screw in the pulley bolts without tightening.
