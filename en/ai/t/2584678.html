Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 3330C40 BRAKE FLUID RESERVOIR CAP - R + R.

Using a syringe, drain the brake fluid from the reservoir.

1. Remove the retaining clip.

2. Use tool (2a) to undo connectors (2b), then remove rigid pipe (2c).

If servicing the other pipe, use a fixed 13 mm wrench to remove connector (2d).
