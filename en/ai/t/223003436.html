Functional description

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The supply for the body computer M01 is both controlled by the ignition and direct to pin 11 of connector E and pin A of connector C of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 .

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

There is a protective fuse F1 for the direct supply located in the engine compartment junction unit B01 .

Connector B for the body computer represents the interface for connecting the external diagnostic equipment (Examiner).

The system is capable of testing numerous control units:

  • ABS from pin 1 of connector B
  • Air Bag from pin 3 of connector B and pin 12 of connector E
  • Engine management control unit pin 7 of connector B
  • CODE function pin 8 of connector B
  • Engine control unit from pin 20 of connector C

Pins 4 and 5 of connector B and 8 and 9 of connector D represent the earth references.
