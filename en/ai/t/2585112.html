Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Supporting the opening flap appropriately, lift up the safety lever (1a) and separate the ends (1b) of the telescopic strut from the pins (1c), the hinge (1d) and the support (1e).

2. Remove the telescopic strut.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the telescopic strut fitting the ends on the pins on the hinges and the support.

Close the safety lever for the telescopic strut ends.
