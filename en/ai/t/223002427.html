Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Doblò trim level and MY 2005

The vehicle is available, on request, with a car radio system.

The system consists of the following components:

- car radio (CD / cassette player);

- radio supply leads;

- two front speakers located in the doors and two in the dashboard;

- two rear speakers;

- two rear speakers;

- cables connecting the radio with the speakers;

- aerial;

- coaxial aerial lead.

The car radio system has a communication protocol with the electronic unit that manages the electrical equipment (body computer).

When the radio is supplied the body computer reads the radio code and establishes whether or not it is activated.

The radio circuit is supplied with the key ON and via a direct supply from the battery (which allows tuning, safety codes, etc. to be stored).

The two power supply lines are protected by three fuses two of which are housed in the junction unit under the dashboard and the third is located in the engine compartment junction unit.
