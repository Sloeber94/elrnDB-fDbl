1820A26 thrust bearing linkage - r + r with gearbox removed

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the circlip (1a) retaining the clutch release lever (1b) and remove the lever.

2. Remove the top bush.

3. Withdraw the fork control shaft.

4. Remove the bottom bush.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the condition of the removed components.

Replace the bushes, if there is too much play on the fork shaft.

Refit the bottom bush.

Refit the fork control shaft.

Refit the top bush.

Position the clutch release lever and secure it to the fork shaft using the specific circlip.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

Refitting

2. Remove the lever.

3. Remove the outer bush.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

1. Remove the thrust control linkage.

2. Remove the inner bush.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

Refitting

Removing

- Place the inner bush back in its housing.

- Place the thrust control linkage back in its housing.

- Place the outer bush back in its housing.

- Place the lever back in its housing and secure it using the circlip.
