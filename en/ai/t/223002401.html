Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Right hand drive and Cargo trim level and MY 2005

The radio may be equipped with a radio as an option.

The system consists of the following components:

- radio (CD/tape player);

- radio supply leads;

- two front speakers on the facia;

- aerial;

- coaxial aerial lead.

The radio system is equipped with a communication protocol with the electronic unit that manages the electric system (body computer).

When the radio is supplied with power, the body computer reads the radio code and determines whether to activate it.

The radio circuit is supplied with the key ON and via a direct supply from the battery (which allows tuning, safety codes, etc. to be stored).

The above line is protected by two fuses in the engine compartment junction unit and the under-dashboard junction unit.
