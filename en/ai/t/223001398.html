Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The earths present on the vehicle, categorised according to their relationship with the various wires, are as follows:

- battery negative wiring: A01 D, C02, C03;

- front wiring: C10, C12;

- radiator wiring: C01 B, C01 C;

- dashboard wiring: C15 A, C50 A;

- rear wiring: C15 C, C30, C50 B;

- engine wiring: C01 A, C11, C39, C40, C40 A, C40 B, C60, C60 A, C60 B, C62.

The earths in the engine wiring depend on the relevant engine version.
