I10 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS / NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, when the steering column switch unit lever is raised or lowered, the direction indicators do not work. When the button is pressed, the hazard warning lights do not work

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH EXAMINER DIAGNOSTIC INSTRUMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the EXAMINER diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the Body Computer.

Continue to Step 2

Direction indicators error. Incorrect bulb

Check condition of bulbs

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

Front bulb damaged

Replace the bulb Op. 5550C18 bulb of one front turn signal, l/h or r/h - r+r

Damaged rear bulb

Replace the bulb Op. 5550C26 l/h or r/h side turn signal bulb - r+r

Side repeater damaged

Replace the side repeater Op. 5550C22 l/h or r/h side flasher (one) - r+r

Rear bulb holder assembly faulty

Repair or replace the rear lights cluster Op. 5540A44 l/h or r/h tail light (one) - r+r

Front bulb holder assembly faulty

Repair or change the front light unit Op. 5540B79 right front light cluster with turn signal and foglamp r r

3

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly and check that the direction indicators switch is working properly Characteristic of working principle 5550A stalk unit

Continue to Step 4

Steering column switch unit not working properly

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

4

CHECK HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the hazard warning lights switch See E2020

Continue to Step 5

Hazard warning lights switch faulty

Replace the control panel Op. 5505A24 w/l and service switch mount - r+r

5

CHECK DIRECTION INDICATORS/HAZARD LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between stalk unit and Body Computer
  • connections between Body Computer and junction unit
  • connections between lights and Body Computer
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs;
  • bulb earth See E2020

Continue to Step 6

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E2020

6

CHECK ON BODY COMPUTER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Body Computer is supplied correctly

Continue to Step 7

Incorrect supply

Restore the correct supply : See E2020

Body Computer faulty

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
