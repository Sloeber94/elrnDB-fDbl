Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The adjustment of the two door mirrors is achieved by means of the special control device located near the driver's mirror.

Both mirrors are adjusted by means of a single button which can be pressed in four directions: this is made possible by a switch that allows either the left or the right mirror to be selected.

The button controls two electrical motors located in each of the two mirrors: one motor allows the rotation of the mirror on a horizontal axis, the other on a vertical axis.

The rear view mirror adjustment circuit is protected by a fuse located in the junction unit that also protects other ignition-controlled power supplies.

The adjustment can only take place with the ignition ON.
