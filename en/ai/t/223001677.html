Functional description

The supply for the front courtesy light G10 reaches pin 2 via the body computer M01 (pin 23 connector A). The earth signal for switching on the courtesy light, arrives:

  • from the left front door switch I16;
  • from the right front door switch I17;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The supply for the body computer M01 is both controlled by the ignition and direct to pin 11 of connector E and pin A of connector C of the junction unit under the dashboard B02 . There is a protective fuse F1 for the direct supply located in the engine compartment junction unit B01 .

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Pins 8 and 9 connector D of the body computer are connected to earth.

The body computer manages the supply for the rear courtesy light G20 at pin 33 connector A.

The rear courtesy light G20 is connected at pin 3:

  • to the tailgate lock I21 at pin 3; (pin 1 of I11 );

Both courtesy lights are earthed at pin 1.
