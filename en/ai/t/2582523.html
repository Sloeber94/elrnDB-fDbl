0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check for leaks from or damage to the fuel tank.

Continue to Step 1

Leaks from the fuel tank

Repair the fuel tank, or replace if necessary Op. 1040A14 fuel tank - r + r for replacement

1

CHECK TANK FILLER FITTING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tank filler fitting, in particular:

  • The seal of the filler housing on the wing;
  • The condition of the filler;
  • The cap seal

Continue to Step 2

Filler faulty

Renew or replace the filler Op. 1040A31 fuel filler pipe - rigid section - r r

2

CHECK PUMP COVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fuel pump/gauge cover gasket seal

Continue to Step 3

Seal worn, cover damaged

Replace the gasket or cover Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

3

CHECK BOOT FRAME SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that boot is sealed correctly.

Continue to Step 4

Boot seal faulty

Restore correct boot seal

4

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs in the boot area are present

Continue to Step 5

Underbody plugs missing

Refit missing plugs
