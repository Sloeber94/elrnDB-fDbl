Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is available, on request, with preparation for the installation of a mobile phone.

This preparation is configured as follows:

- dual function aerial (car radio and mobile phone);

- hands-free speaker, located in the dashboard on the passenger side (it is the same speaker for the radio with two connections: one for the radio and one for the mobile phone hands-free function);

- hands-free microphone located inside the front courtesy light.

The connection for the mobile phone has two power supplies: one direct from the battery and one only with the ignition ON.

The two lines are protected by fuses in the junction unit under the battery and in the engine compartment.
