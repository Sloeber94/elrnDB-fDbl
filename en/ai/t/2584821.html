2. Slacken, without removing, the bottom bolts securing the air conditioner compressor.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

If the compressor is being replaced, it is available as a spare part containing the recommended amount of fluid.

For this reason, before fitting it on the vehicle, the amount of fluid which corresponds to that remaining in the system must be removed.

In order to do this it is necessary to:

1. Drain the fluid contained in the compressor through the hole for attaching the pipe from the air conditioner compressor to the condenser. If necessary, rotate the compressor several times, manually, to remove all the fluid.

The fluid is extremely hygroscopic: avoid leaving the containers open. Avoid leaving the compressor or any other component disconnected from the system for longer than is strictly necessary.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

1. Pour the quantity of fluid (1a) present in the compressor to be replaced in a graduated test tube (1b), taking care to let the contents drain well.

2. Pour the quantity of fluid (2a) present in the new compressor in a graduated test tube (2b), taking care to let the contents drain well.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

1. Remove the excess amount of fluid (1a) corresponding to the difference between the quantity of fluid contained in the test tube (1b) and the test tube (1c).

2. Introduce the remaining amount of oil in the new compressor.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
