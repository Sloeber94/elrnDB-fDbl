Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

FAN

The two-speed cooling fan makes it possible to increase heat dissipation by the engine and/or the air conditioning system.

The fan is controlled by the fuel injection E.C.U. in accordance with a strategy for heat dissipation and maintenance of working temperature (for operating logic, see 1056)

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

ELECTRIC FAN

The two speed electric cooling fan makes it possible to increase the dissipation of the heat from the radiator and/or the air conditioning system condenser.

It is controlled directly by the injection control unit in accordance with a specific operating sequence (see 1056).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

FANS

The two-speed electric cooling fans increase the heat dissipation capacity of the radiator and/or air conditioning condenser.

They are controlled directly by the injection control unit in accordance with a specific operating logic

See descriptions 1060 DIESEL INJECTION SYSTEM
