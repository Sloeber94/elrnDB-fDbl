The control button operates the passenger compartment air fan normally, but this air is at the outside temperature and has not been cooled.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

BELT CONDITION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

1.2 8v

Check the condition of the compressor drive belt and make sure the tension is correct.

Continue to Step 2

Belt defective

Replace the belt Op. 1092D10 air conditioner compressor drive belt - r + r

Tension incorrect

Restore correct tension Op. 1092D14 air conditioner compressor drive belt - adjust tension

1.9 DIESEL

Check the condition of the compressor drive belt and make sure the tension is correct.

Continue to Step 2

Belt defective

Replace the belt Op. 1092G10 single engine belt r r

2

CHECK ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Compressor fuse
  • Continuity and insulation of climate control electrical circuit See E6020

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace fuse (and search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore connections

3

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket and carry out active diagnosis of the compressor relay.

Continue to Step 4

Relay faulty

Replace the relay

4

CLIMATE CONTROL COMPONENTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • compressor activation control
  • 4 stage pressure switch See Test 5040BA Check operation of 4 stage pressure switch
  • electronic thermostat

Continue to Step 5

Compressor control faulty

Replace the compressor control Op. 5040A22

4 stage pressure switch faulty

Replace the pressure switch Op. 5040B14 air conditioner system multi-level pressure switch - r+r

Electronic thermostat faulty

Replace the electronic thermostat Op. 5040B22 air conditioning system de-mister thermostat - r+r

5

CHECK COMPRESSOR ELECTRO-MAGNET COUPLING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the compressor electro-magnet coupling. In particular, check the resistance, coil, supply and earth.

Continue to Step 6

Electro-magnetic coupling malfunction

Replace the compressor Op. 5040A20 air conditioner compressor - r+r - includes fluid level check and top up if necessary

6

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the climate control system to the repressurizing system and check the system pressure See Test 5040B Check on operation of air conditioning system (pressures)

Continue to Step 7

The pressures do not alter much when the compressor is switched on

Replace the compressor Op. 5040A20 air conditioner compressor - r+r - includes fluid level check and top up if necessary

The high and low pressures vary, but do not reach the correct values.

Repressurize the system Op. 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling

Evaporator dirty

Replace the evaporator Op. 5040C10 air conditioner box - r+r
