1042A02 C.N.G. - FUEL SYSTEM Safety check

In the case of interventions on components of the CNG system, it is necessary, for safety reasons, to carry out the following procedures to drain the high and/or low pressure systems.

PROCEDURE FOR DRAINING HIGH PRESSURE CIRCUIT

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Shut the manual valves located on the CNG cylinder assembly.

Disconnect the electrical connection of the pressure sensor located on the pressure reducer.

Using the CNG/methane switch located on the central tunnel, select the Methane starting mode.

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Start the engine and wait for it to stall.

Connect the electrical connection of the pressure sensor.

Connect the Examiner diagnostic station and check that the methane pressure in the pipe is nearly zero.

Using the Examiner, delete any errors stored in the control unit's memory.

PROCEDURE FOR DRAINING LOW PRESSURE CIRCUIT

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Disconnect the connection of the methane cut-off solenoid located on the reduction unit.

Using the CNG/methane switch located on the central tunnel, select Methane starting mode.

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Start the engine and wait for it to stall.

Repeat the engine starting and wait for it to stall again, to eliminate the residual gas in the pipes.

Improper engine stalling could cause the control unit to store certain errors in memory (petrol pump relay error, injector relay error, etc.).

Proceed with the service intervention to be conducted on the vehicle, making sure to wear the special helmet with visor conforming to UNI standards.

When unscrewing the connectors, even if the internal pressure has discharged, make sure you do not come directly into contact with the jet of CNG present in the pipe.

At the end of the service intervention, remember to delete any errors in the control unit's memory.
