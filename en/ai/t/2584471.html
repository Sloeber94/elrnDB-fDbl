Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

1. Undo the retaining bolts on the reinforcing bracket on the oil sump.

2. Remove the reinforcement bracket.

3. Undo the nuts (3a) fixing the exhaust pipe (3b) to the manifold.

4. Recover the gasket between exhaust pipe and manifold.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with the removal 7055B54 under-engine guard - r r .

1. Undo nuts (1a) fixing the exhaust pipe attachment to the engine (1b), to the sleeve and lower as required.

2. Remove the exhaust pipe attachment to the engine gasket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
