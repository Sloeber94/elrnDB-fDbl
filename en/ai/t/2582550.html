Results of the tests

1

INTAKE AND EXHAUST SYSTEM LEAKAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the intake and exhaust system, checking that there are no leakd downstream of the MAP sensor/flow meter. See Test 1072A Intake and exhaust system leakage check

Also take into account:

  • blow-by circuit;
  • canister circuit, checking that the valve is not stuck open;
  • brake servo vacuum pipes;
  • Injector O-Rings

Continue to Step 2

Leaks are present

Eliminate leaks from the system. Replace faulty parts if necessary

Continue to Step 6

2

PETROL PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Turn the key to MAR and check fuel pump activates.

While the pump is active, check fuel pressure is not too low

Continue to Step 3

Low pressure: fuel filter blocked/obstructed

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1070A40

Continue to Step 6

Low pressure: supply pipe restrictions/leaks

Renew or, if necessary, replace the pipes involved

Low pressure: pressure regulator defective

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1070A40

Continue to Step 6

Low pressure: pressure regulator incorrectly calibrated

Calibrate correctly.

Continue to Step 6

Low pressure: electrical problem in the fuel pump

Restore correct connections See E5030If necessary, replace the fuel pump

Continue to Step 6

Low pressure: excess pressure valve stuck open

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Continue to Step 6

3

CHECK ON FUEL INJECTORS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the mechanical seal of the injectors.

In particular, check that none of the injectors are stuck closed

Continue to Step 4

Injectors defective

Renew the correct seal for the injectors, if necessary replace them Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

Continue to Step 6

4

CHECKING FUEL QUALITY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is no water or other substances in the tank which is contaminating the fuel.

Continue to Step 5

Impurities are present

Clean the tank Op. 1040A10 fuel tank - r + r and decant fueland introduce new fuel.

Continue to Step 6
