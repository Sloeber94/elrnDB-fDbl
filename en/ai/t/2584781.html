Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a lift.

Remove a front wheel, proceed as in the example

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R..

Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (ONE) - R.R..

Op. 3310A57 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER BRACKET (ONE) - R.R..

Op. 3310A50 FRONT BRAKE DISCS - R.R..

Remove the brake disc shield.

Op. 4450C10 FRONT STEERING KNUCKLE (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT COMPLETE WITH WHEEL HUB - R.R. WITH WHEEL REMOVED - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY.

Op. 4450C34 FRONT WHEEL HUB BEARING, LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE WITH STEERING KNUCKLE REMOVED.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Op. 4450C34 FRONT WHEEL HUB BEARING, LEFT OR RIGHT - REPLACE WITH STEERING KNUCKLE REMOVED.

Op. 4450C10 FRONT STEERING KNUCKLE (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT COMPLETE WITH WHEEL HUB - R.R. WITH WHEEL REMOVED - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY.

Fit the brake disc shield.

Op. 3310A50 FRONT BRAKE DISCS - R.R..

Op. 3310A57 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER BRACKET (ONE) - R.R..

Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (ONE) - R.R..

Refit the front wheel, proceed as in the example

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R..

Remove the car from the lift.
