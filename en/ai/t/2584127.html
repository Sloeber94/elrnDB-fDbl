3330C41 brake fluid resevoir cap - r r for versions with abs systems .

Drain the fluid from the brake fluid reservoir.

Position containers to collect brake fluid under the front and rear wheels.

Open the valves on the front brake calipers and the rear wheel cylinders or calipers, and drain the fluid contained in the system.

Close the valves on the front brake calipers and rear wheel cylinders.

Top up the level of fluid in the reservoir, checking that it is between the MIN and MAX marks.
