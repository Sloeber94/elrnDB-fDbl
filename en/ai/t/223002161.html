Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: 7 seater and MY 2005

Ignition-operated power supply (INT) protected by fuse F49 of under-dashboard control unit B02 reaches the incorrect second row seat position indication switch I141 (N.O.).

The incorrect third row seat position indication switch I142 (N.C.) is connected in line with the incorrect second row seat position indication switch I141. when the seats in the second row are tilted and the seats in the third row are in normal position, switch I141 supplies power to incorrect seat position indication buzzer E81.

Buzzer E81 is connected to left rear earth C30.
