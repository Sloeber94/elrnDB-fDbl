Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove flap (1b) from its seating.

Take care not to damage the body paint.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Sliding door

1. Disconnect the cable (1a) locking the sliding side door with the fuel flap open from its seating (1b) on the fuel flap.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Sliding door

Refitting

Removing

1. Connect the cable (1a) locking the sliding side door with the fuel flap open, to its seating (1b) on the fuel flap, inserting the centring devices (1c) located on the cable, in the slots (1d) in the fuel flap seating.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Sliding door

Fit the flap and secure it using the bolts.
