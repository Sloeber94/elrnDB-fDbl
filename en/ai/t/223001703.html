Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Cargo trim level and Sliding door and MY 2005

The power supply for the front courtesy light G10 reaches pin 2 via the body computer M01.

The body computer M01 manages the power supply and the timed delay - respectively from pins 23 and 21 of connector A - via an operating logic determined by the input signals at connector A of the body computer, coming:

- from the switch for the left front door lock geared motor N50, to pin 8;

- from the switch for the right front door lock geared motor N51, to pin 25.

The body computer M01 receives a supply controlled by the ignition and a direct supply at pins 11 of connector E nd pin A of connector C, respectively of the junction unit under the dashboard B02.

There is a proective fuse F1 for the direct power supply located in the engine compartment junction unit B01.

Pins 8 and 9 of connector D of the body computer are earthed.

The supply for the rear courtesy light G20 - pin 2 - arrives from pin 33 connector A of the body computer via the line protected by fuse F39 of the junction unit under the dashboard B02.

Pin 3 of the rear courtesy light G20 is connected to the switch on the rear door I21 (boot opening switch I11 in the version with a swing door) and to the switches on the sliding side doors D196 and D197 through which they receive the earth signals when the tailgate or the side doors are opened.

Both the courtesy lights are earthed via pin 1.
