Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, when the appropriate button on the control panel is pressed, the heated rear window does not warm up properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON REAR WINDOW EARTH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the heated rear window is connected to earth properly See E3030

Continue to Step 2

Connection not correct

Restore connection

2

CHECK ON HEATING ELEMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the heated rear window demisting heating element

Continue to Step 3

Heating element broken

Replace the rearscreen Op. 7035B24 tinted window of one load compartment rear door flap r r including cleaning of housing on flap

3

SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the heated rear window is supplied properly: the battery voltage must be at the supply cable.

Continue to Step 4

No supply

See table J20 REARSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING NOT WORKING

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK
