Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

7055B54 under-engine guard - r r

1. Loosen the lower alternator retaining nut.

2. Loosen the alternator upper nut.

3. Rotate the alternator clockwise and remove the belt

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check belt condition. In particular, check for tears, cracks, surface wear and dry or hard parts that could lead to loss of grip. Change the belt if one of these defects is noted.

Fit the new alternator drive belt on the pullies.

Rotate the alternator anticlockwise and secure it with the retaining bolts.

1. Using tool (1a) check the belt tension (1b) is within recommended values.
