SPEED GEARS CHECK

Operation

Overhaul the gearbox Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings. In particular, check the teeth of the gear concerned: they should not be worn or show signs of seizing

Continue to Step 2

Gears worn or damaged

Replace the gears involved Op. 2110B32 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers, inner controls, gears, shafts and bearings

All OK
