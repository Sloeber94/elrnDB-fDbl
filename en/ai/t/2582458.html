Restore connections

The climate control can only be switched on when the knob is in position 1, 2, 3 or 4. It cannot be switched on in position 0

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING COMPRESSOR CONTROL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using an ohmmeter, check the compressor engagement control.

in the OFF position circuit should be open, whilst in the ON position it should be short circuited

Continue to Step 2

Control defective

Replace the control Op. 5040D22 control (one) of air conditioner control support - arrange

2

CHECKING FAN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the passenger compartment air fan.

Continue to Step 3

Fan faulty

Replace the fan Op. 5040C30 fan motor - r+r

3

CHECKING ADJUSTMENT RESISTANCES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the passenger compartment air fan speed adjustment resistances. See Test 5040CC Check fan motor speed selector operation

Continue to Step 4

Adjustment resistance circuit open

Replace the resistor Op. 5040C48 a/c fan motor resistor - r+r

4

CHECKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the connecting cables between the passenger compartment air fan control, the adjustment resistances and the fan motor See E6020

Continue to Step 5

Connections not correct

Restore connections

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
