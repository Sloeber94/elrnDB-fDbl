Operation

Preliminarily carry out a visual inspect to locate the component or area which is leaking.

OIL SUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • the condition of the oil sump;
  • the seal of the sealant between the sump and the crankcase
  • that the bolts fixing the sump are correctly tightened
