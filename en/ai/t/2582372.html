B25 THE ENGINE STARTS UP AND CUTS OUT AFTER A FEW SECONDS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

When the engine fuel system selector is set at METHANE, the engine starts up normally using petrol but cuts out after a few seconds and does not switch to METHANE.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER
