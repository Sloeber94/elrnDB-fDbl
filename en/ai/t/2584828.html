Refitting

Op. 5040A10 AIR CONDITIONING REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT - DRYING AND REPRESSURIZING

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1. Undo the fixing nut, expansion valve side.

2. Undo the fixing nut (2a), connector side, and remove the pipe (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged.

Fit the pipe in its seating and tighten the fixing nuts on the expansion valve side and connector side.

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Refitting

Op. 5040A10 AIR CONDITIONING REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT - DRYING AND REPRESSURIZING

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a), disconnect the pipe (1b) and move aside the expansion tank (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

1. Undo the nut (1a) and disconnect the pipes (1b) from the compressor to the expansion valve, intermediate connector side.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing nut (1a) and disconnect the pipe (1b), expansion valve side.

Remove the pipe

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged.

If it is, replace the pipe.

Connect the pipe on the expansion valve side and secure it with the nut.

Connect the pipe to the section of pipe connected to the compressor, and secure them both to the intermediate connector with the relevant nut.

Fit the expansion tank and secure it with its bolts.

Connect the water supply pipe from the engine to the expansion tank, expansion tank side.

Connect the battery negative terminal.
