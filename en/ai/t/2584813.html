Op. 7040A10 INSTRUMENT PANEL TRIM - R.R.

1. Disconnect the flexible cable (1a), heater side, releasing it from the control (1b) through the slot (1c) located on the control.

2. Release the flexible transmission cable from the retainer.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the flexible transmission cable (1a), mounting side, undo the fixing bolt (1b) and release the cable from the control lever (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the condition of the flexible cable.

Connect the flexible cable to the control lever on the mounting and secure it with its bolt.

Connect the flexible cable to the control lever on the heater and secure it to the retainer located on the heater.

1. Operate the slide switch on the air intake control and check that the flap is operating correctly by carrying out some opening/closing cycles.
