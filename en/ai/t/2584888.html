Release the wiring from the retaining springs.

Before removing the electrical wiring from the housing it is advisable to connect a general sensor to each individual connector so that it retraces the route of the section of wiring removed. This operation makes it possible, during the refitting stage, to restore the correct routing of the wiring removed.

Disconnect the windscreen washer fluid supply pipe and remove the electrical wiring from the rear of the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring is not damaged.

If necessary, check the continuity.

Connect each individual electrical connector to the sensor used previusly in order to facilitate the routing of the section of cable removed in its original position.

Pull the end of the sensor and restore the routing of the electrical wiring.

Carry out the same operation for all the electrical connectors.

Position the electrical wiring, securing it using new retaining bands.

Disconnect each individual electrical connector from the sensor.
