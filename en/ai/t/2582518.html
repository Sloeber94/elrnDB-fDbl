Whilst the seat belt is rewinding/winding there is a noise coming from the reel

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

REEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the reel and that it is correctly fitted.

Check the reel angular lock.

Check the reel inertia lock

Continue to Step 2

Fitting not correct

See table O31 SEAT BELTS LOCKED

Angular lock not working

Replace the seat belt Op. 7065A10 , (One), front SEAT BELT left or right - R.R.

Inertia lock not working

Replace the seat belt Op. 7065A10 , (One), front SEAT BELT left or right - R.R.

2

HEIGHT ADJUSTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test on a straight road with an even surface to check that there is no noise coming from the seat belt area

Continue to Step 3

Noise coming from the height adjuster: fitting not correct

Refit correctly Op. 7065A10 , (One), front SEAT BELT left or right - R.R.

Noise coming from the height adjuster: component faulty

Replace the damaged components Op. 7065A18 Front set belt HEIGHT ADJUSTER , (one), left or right - R.R.

Noise coming from the buckle: fitting not correct

Loosen the fixing bolts, correctly position the references and tighten to the recommended torque

Noise coming from the buckle: fixing not correct

Restore the correct fixing If necessary, replace the belt Op. 7065A14 ANCHORAGE ROD for , (one), front seat belt left or right - R.R.

Noise coming from the buckle: component faulty

Replace the damaged components Op. 7065A14 ANCHORAGE ROD for , (one), front seat belt left or right - R.R.

Noise coming from the buckle: foreign bodies inside the button

Remove foreign bodies
