Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: Doblò trim level and MY 2005

The power supply to front courtesy light G10 reaches pin 2 through body computer M01.

Body computer M01 manages the power supply and timing - from pins 23 and 21 of connector A respectively - through an operating strategy determined by input signals to connector A of the body computer from:

- left front door lock gear motor switch N50, at pin 8;

- right front door lock gear motor switch N51, at pin 25;

The body computer M01 receives a supply controlled by the ignition and a direct supply at pin 11 of connector E and pin A of connector C respectively of under-dashboard junction unit B02.

There is a proective fuse F1 for the direct power supply located in the engine compartment junction unit B01.

Pins 8 and 9 of connector D of the body computer are earthed.

A power supply leaves pin 33 connector A of the body computer for rear courtesy light G20 - pin 2 - through a line protected by fuse F39 of under-dashboard junction unit B02.

This line also supplies boot courtesy light G40.

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Pin 3 of rear courtesy light G20 is connected to the switch on the rear door flap I21 (boot opening switch I11, in the version with up and over door) and to the switches on sliding side doors D196 and D197, through with they receive earth signals upon the opening of the side-hinged door/tailgate or side doors.

Both courtesy lights are earthed through pin 1.
