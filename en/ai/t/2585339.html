1042AB Check on operation of methane cut out solenoid valve on canister

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

1) Select methane operation.

2) Keeping the engine on, raise the vehicle on the lift.

3) Close all the canister taps except for one (work in order starting with the one on the left).

4) Wait for about 1 minute.

5) If the vehicle does not switch over to petrol start again from point 3) leaving another tap open and keeping all the others closed.

6) If the vehicle switches over to petrol this means that the canister solenoid valve with the tap open is stuck closed. Then proceed to activate it through the active diagnosis, check the wiring/connections and, if necessary, check the coil resistance.
