7015A28 l/h or r/h tailgate telescopic strut (one) - r+r

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the tailgate and support.

2. Using a screwdriver, prise off (without detaching) the retaining clips on both sides.

3. Remove the telescopic strut.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Doblò trim level

Refitting

Removing

Check that the telescopic strut is undamaged.

Refit the telescopic strut, press-fitting the ends of the anchoring pins.

Fit the retaining clips on both ends.

Check that the telescopic strut works properly.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: 7 seater

Removing

Refitting

Open the tailgate and support it using a prop.

1. Tighten bolts (1a) and remove panel half-shells (1b) on the upper tailgate strut attachment.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the guard bracket (2b) for the lower telescopic strut on the body.

3. Using a screwdriver, prise off (without detaching) the retaining clips on both sides.

4. Prise the telescopic strut from its seat and remove.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: 7 seater

Refitting

Removing

Check that the telescopic strut is undamaged.

Refit the telescopic strut, press-fitting the ends of the anchoring pins.

Fit the retaining clips on both ends.

Position the lower attachment guard bracket in its seat and tighten both bolts.

Position the half-shells in their seats on the upper attachment and secure with bolts.

Remove the support applied previously.

Check that the telescopic strut works properly.
