Test the mechanism with someone sitting on the seat.

Operation

Presence of obstacles or dirt alogn the device mechanism

Lever or retaining elements broken

Replace the lever Op. 7045A26or other affected parts

Restore the correct fastening

CHECKING SEAT LIFT DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the seat Op. 7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r. Check the seat lifting device In particular, check the condition of the device gear teeth and ensure it is properly secured.

Continue to Step 4

Device faulty

Repair or replace the device Op. 7045A34 manual device for adjusting seat height - r r with seat removed.
