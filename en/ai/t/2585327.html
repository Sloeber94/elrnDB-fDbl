Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Measure the pump supply voltage at the pump connector.

Meaure the pump absorption current by positioning the ammeter clip for the Examiner on the pump supply cable (on the fuse box or directly on the pump inlet cable.

The current measured should be between 2 and 10 Ampere.
