Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

All electrical systems and circuits are supplied by battery A01.

The power supply for the various devices is managed by the two junction units in the engine compartment B01 and under the dashboard B02 that contain the fuses and the relays that allow the corrcet operation of the electrical equipment.

Other circuits are supplied by turning the ignition key H01 to various positions:

- in the ON position, numerous circuits and services receive an ignition-operated power supply protected (INT line) by the fuses in the junction unit B01 (line 15/54);

- the starter motor (line 50) is operated in AVV position;

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

- the side lights (STAZ and POS lines) are supplied in PARK position.

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS
