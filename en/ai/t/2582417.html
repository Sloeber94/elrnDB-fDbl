Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, the Body Computer does not detect faults in the operation of the side lights, stop lights or rear fog lamps, despite the fact that they are not working, and the 'Light failure' warning light does not come on

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

CHECK ON BODY COMPUTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the 'lights check' device inside the Body Computer is working properly

Continue to Step 2

Light check device not working properly

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

All OK

Problems met

  • Body Computer supply; See E1010

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore connections See E1010

Incorrect supply
