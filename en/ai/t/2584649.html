3330B23 brake pedal - r r for rhd versions

Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet.

Disconnect the positive battery terminal.

Op. 3330B27 CLUTCH PEDAL - R.R. FOR RIGHT HAND DRIVE VERSIONS

1. Remove the brake pedal switch

2. Remove the brake servo push rod retaining spring

3. Undo the nut fixing the brake pedal idler hinge pin.

4. Completely withdraw the pin (4a) supporting the brake and clutch pedals and remove the brake pedal (4b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Refitting

Removing

Position the brake pedal and fit the pin supporting the brake and clutch pedals.

Tighten the nut fixing the brake pedal idler hinge pin.

Position the brake servo push rod retaining spring.

Position the brake pedal switch

Op. 3330B27 CLUTCH PEDAL - R.R. FOR RIGHT HAND DRIVE VERSIONS

Connect the positive battery terminal.

Close the bonnet lid.
