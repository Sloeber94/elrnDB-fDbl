J03 SCREEN WASHER NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the right lever on the stalk unit is operated towards the steering wheel, the windscreen washer function does not work properly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the detergent fluid level in the reservoir Also make sure that the reservoir is not leaking

Continue to Step 1

Fluid level insufficient

Restore the correct level Op. 5050A14 reservoir plug (in engine bay) or windscreen washer fluid level sensor - check and top up if necessary

Reservoir damaged

Replace the reservoir Op. 5050A10 windscreen washer fluid reservoir (in engine bay) - r+r

1

PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the pipes. In particular check that the pipes are not obstructed or connected incorrectly

Also check the condition of the jets

Continue to Step 2

Pipes obstructed or damaged

Renew or replace the pipes

Jets obstructed or damaged

Renew or replace the jets Op. 5050B10 front windscreen washer jets - r+r clean and/or position

2

CHECK ON WINDSCREEN WASHER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Windscreen washer fuse on dashboard unit
  • Connection between fuse and stalk unit
  • Connection between stalk unit and two-way windscreen washer pump See E3020

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore connections

3

CHECK ON TWO-WAY PUMP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the two-way windscreen/rearscree washer pump is working

Continue to Step 4

Fault in the electric pump

Replace the electric pump Op. 5550B22

4

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly: in particular the lever for the windscreen washer function Characteristic of working principle 5550A stalk unit

Continue to Step 5

Fault in steering column switch unit

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

5
