'INSTRUMENT PANEL' CONTROL UNITS TEST USING EXAMINER DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the active diagnosis of the fuel gauge. The fuel gauge indicator should reach the end of the scale.

Continue to Step 2

The indicator does not move or moves unevenly

Replace the instrument panel Op. 5560B11 analogue control panel - r+r or replace

2

'BODY COMPUTER' CONTROL UNITS TEST USING EXAMINER DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the active diagnosis of the 'fuel level (CAN)'.

The fuel level indicator should move

Continue to Step 3

The indicator does not move

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

3

CHECK ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the wiring between the fuel level sensor and the Body Computer See E4010

Continue to Step 4

Cables broken

Renew the wiring See E4010

Cables short circuited

4

FUEL LEVEL SENSOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the fuel level sensor

Continue to Step 5

Sensor not working

Replace the fuel pump/level sensor assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r
