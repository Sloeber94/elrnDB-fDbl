Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is equipped with two extra-penetration fog lamps for use in the case of poor visibility.

The foglamps are operated by means of a button on the control unit in the centre of the dashboard.

The fog lamp circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a relay, both located in the underfacia junction unit.

The sidelights must be switched on in order to switch the fog lights on.

If the ignition key is switched OFF or the side lights are turned off, the fog lights are deactivated even if the operating button is still pressed:

A warning light on the instrument panel indicates foglamp activation.
