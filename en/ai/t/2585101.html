Op. 7025A24 BONNET LID OPENING LEVER - R+R

It is advisable to connect to the flexible transmission cable, bonnet opening lever side, an ordinary probe, which retraces the cable's route.

Release the transmission cable from the retaining bands.

Remove the transmission cable from its seating, working from the bonnet lock side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the flexible transmission cable is not damaged.

Connect the end of the transmission cable, bonnet opening lever side, to the probe and return the cable to its correct layout.

Secure the flexible cable to the retaining bands.
