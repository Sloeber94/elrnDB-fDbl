Refitting

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Op. 7020E34 REAR DOOR FLAP INTERIOR PANEL - R+R

1. Disconnect the rigid control for opening/closing the door via the outer handle, lock side.

2. Disconnect the electrical connector for the door open warning light switch.
