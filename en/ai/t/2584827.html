Op. 1044B14 MOUNTING FOR FUEL FILTER, INJECTION CONTROL UNIT AND HEATER PLUGS CONTROL UNIT - R.R. WITH INTERFERING COMPONENTS REMOVED

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and disconnect the pipe (1b), dehydrating filter side.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged.

If it is, replace the pipe.

Fit the pipe and secure it to the retaining bands.

Connect the pipe to the dehydrating filter and tighten the bolt.

Connect the pipe to the heater and secure it with its nut.

Op. 1044B14 MOUNTING FOR FUEL FILTER, INJECTION CONTROL UNIT AND HEATER PLUGS CONTROL UNIT - R.R. WITH INTERFERING COMPONENTS REMOVED
