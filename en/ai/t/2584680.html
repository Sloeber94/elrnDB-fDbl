Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a lift.

Op. 3330C40 BRAKE FLUID RESERVOIR CAP - R + R.

Draw off brake fluid from the reservoir using a syringe.

Raise the lift.

1. Use wrench (1a) to unscrew rear brake pipe connector (b) from the cylinder.

Name

Box wrench for brake fittings

1.856.132.000

2. Remove anchorage clip (2a) and unscew connector (2b) connecting the rigid pipe to the intermediate hose using wrench (2c).

Name

Country

2c

Box wrench for brake fittings

1.856.132.000

3. Release the pipe from the retaining clips on the rear axle, then remove the pipe.

Plug the disconnected pipe ends to prevent fluid emerging.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Fit a new rigid pipe.

When positioning the new rigid pipe, take care not to damage or bend.

Screw the rigid pipe connector to the rear cylinder and tighten.
