1040B36 rear fuel delivery line - r r

Path
Procedures > 10 ENGINE > 1040 FUEL TANK AND PIPES > 1040B FUEL SUPPLY/RETURN CIRCUIT PIPE > 1040B36 rear fuel delivery line - r r
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fDbl/en/#/2584231

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Place the car on a ramp.

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Raise the vehicle.

1. Disconnect the rear section of the fuel pipe from the middle section, acting on the rapid connector.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

1. Position an appropriate stand beneath the tank in order to support it.

2. Undo the tank bracket retaining bolts.

3. Lower the fuel tank (3a) and disconnect the rear pipe (3b) from the fuel pump, then remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v; all production periods

Position the rear section of the fuel pipe, connecting it to the fuel pump.

Raise the tank and tighten the bolts fixing the tank to the bodyshell to torque.

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

1. Disconnect the rapid attachment of the rear fuel supply pipe from the intermediate one.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

1. Place an appropriate stand under the fuel tank to support it during removal.

2. Undo the bolts securing the fuel tank support brackets.

3. Lower the fuel tank (3a) as much as necessary and disconnect the rear fuel supply pipe (3b) from the pump, then remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit the rear fuel delivery pipe in its seat and connect to the pump.

Refit the fuel tank in its seating, then tighten the bolts securing the support brackets to the specified torque.
