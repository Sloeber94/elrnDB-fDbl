Removing

Refitting

The high-pressure circuit has been drained before the removal of the central cylinder.

Before removing the side cylinder, mark its exact position in relation to the bodywork and the retaining bands.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection of the methane cut-off solenoid on the right cylinder.

2. Disconnect the connector on the pipe connecting to the three-way connector.

Place the tool for supporting the cylinders on a hydraulic column lift prepared for supporting the weight of the cylinder.

Arrange the brackets of the cylinder support tool on the innermost holes on the base of the tool, to ensure the best possible grip on the cylinder.

1. Position the tool for supporting the cylinder underneath the right side canister during the removal operation.

2. Undo the nuts (2a), recover the bushes and springs (2b) securing the outside of the cylinder retaining collars.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) on the inside and withdraw the collars (3b) supporting the cylinder.

4. Lower the hydraulic lift and remove the right side cylinder.

With the assistance of a second operator, remove the cylinder from the equipment and rest it on suitable supports.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Cargo trim level and BIPOWER

The complete solenoid must not, under any circumstances, be detached from the CNG cylinder assembly. If there are faults on the solenoid, only the dismantling/reassembly procedure may be carried out 1042A20 injection system solenoid on cylinder r r .
