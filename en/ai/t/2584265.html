Op. 1076B20 REAR EXHAUST SILENCER - R + R.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and release the collar securing the exhaust pipe to the catalyzer (1b).

2. Release the exhaust pipe from the rubber anchoring blocks and disconnect the pipe from the catalyzer.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Do not deform the front hose beyond an angle of X = 20 angle; and the cut Y = 25 mm.

The front flexible pipe must be protected from all exterior mechanical aggression. It must not come into contact with corrosive products; the front flexible pipe will not withstand any deformation when it is in position.

If these precautions are not adhered to, the life of the front flexible pipe will be reduced; the detachment or dismantling of the exhaust line is mandatory for operations which require the propulsion unit to be lifted.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Release the exhaust pipe from the rubber mounting blocks and remove it from the car.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods
