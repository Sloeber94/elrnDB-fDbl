1040A32 fuel filler pipe union sleeve - r + r

Path

Kind

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.4 8v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

Place the car on a ramp.

Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B43 REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1. Unscrew the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the pipe protection bracket.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.4 8v; all production periods

1. Open the band and disconnect the flexible section of the filler pipe from the tank.

2. Open the band and disconnect the flexible section of the filler pipe from the rigid section.

3. Open the plastic band (3a) and remove the flexible section of the fuel filler pipe (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.4 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the flexible section of the fuel filler pipe.

Connect the flexible section of the fuel filler pipe to the tank and to the rigid section.

Fit the pipe protection bracket and tighten the fixing bolts.

Op. 7055B43 REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Lower the vehicle.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 DIESEL; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp.

Proceed with the removal

Op. 7055B43 REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

(Right side).

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the protective bracket (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

1. Loosen the bands (1a) and (1b), open the band (1c) and remove the hose joining the fuel filler pipe to the tank (1d).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the hose joining the fuel filler pipe to the tank back in its housing and fasten it using the bands.

Refit the protective bracket in its seating and secure it with its bolts.

Proceed with the refitting

Op. 7055B43 REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

(Right side).

Remove the car from the ramps.
