CHECKING PIPE AND COMPONENT FASTENINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the compressor and the system pipes and components are correctly fixed.

In the following order:

  • drier filter fixing
  • pipes fixing (with bands and spacers)
  • no interference between the pipes and other components in the engine compartment
  • Compressor fixing to the engine
