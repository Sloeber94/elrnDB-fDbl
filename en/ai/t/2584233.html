Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

1. Undo the plugs (1a) and remove the rear guard (1b).

2. Undo the plugs (2a) and remove the front guard (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the bottom guard and secure it with its retaining plugs.

Fit the rear guard and secure it with its retaining plugs.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.
