Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V, 1.9 DIESEL; production: da 12/03

Description

The vehicle has two additional fog lamps for use in poor visibility conditions. The fog lights are activated through a special button on the switch unit in the middle of the facia.

The fog lights circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a relay, both located in the junction unit in the engine compartment.

The fog lights only come on when the side lights are switched on.

In addition, if the key is turned ot the OFF position or the side lights are switched off, the fog lights are switched off even if the button is pressed.

A warning light in the instrument panel signals that the fog lights have been switched on.
