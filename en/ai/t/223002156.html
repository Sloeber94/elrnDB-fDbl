Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The facia power point P51 and cigar light/power point P50 receive power from relay R12 via a line protected by fuse F44, both contained in under-dashboard junction unit B02.

These lines leave the control unit from pin 9 of connector E and enter pin 2 of P51 and pin 2 of P50.

The power supply of relay R12 is received from the direct line protected by fuse F1 contained in engine compartment junction unit B01 while the coil is excited by ignition switch H01 (INT/A).

The earth is supplied at pin 10 connector E of under-dashboard junction unit B02, at pin 3 of cigar lighter/power point P50 and pin 3 of power point P51.

The ring around facia power point P51 and the right around cigar lighter P50 are lit (both at pin1 of each socket) with the side lights on via an enablement signal leaving body computer M01 and protected by fuse 51 of under-dashboard junction unit B02.
