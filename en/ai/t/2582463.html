The fan which sends air into the passenger compartment does not come on

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

FAN FUSE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the heater fan fuse See E6010

Continue to Step 2

Fuse defective

Replace the fuse (and search for the cause of the problem)

2

FAN RELAY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • The condition of the passenger compartment fan relay
  • Cables connected to the relay See E6010

Continue to Step 3

Relay faulty

Replace the relay

Connections short circuit and/or open circuit

Restore connections

3

FAN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Verify:

  • the condition of the fan operating motor
  • cables connected to the fan

Continue to Step 4

Motor faulty

Replace the motor Op. 5020A30 heater fan motor - r+r

Connecting cables open circuit and/or short circuit

Restore the correct connections

4

FAN CONTROL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity at the passenger compartment air fan control contacts

Continue to Step 5

Fan control defective

Replace the control Op. 5020B22 heater control support control (one) - arrange

Connecting cables open circuit and/or short circuit

Restore the correct connections

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
