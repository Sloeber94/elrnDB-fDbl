Applies to: engines: 1.4 8v, 1.6 16v, 1.3 JTD 16V, 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 12/03; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle climate is controlled via a duct/distributor unit with heater and controlled by means of three control knobs in the dashboard:

- the middle knob controls the mixture flap mechanically between hot air and cold air;

- the left knob mechanically controls the opening of the flaps that regulate the distribution of the air flows by means of a flexible cable;

- the right knob adjusts the ventilationof the air, via an electrical control, at four different speeds; two versions are available: one three speed and one four speed.

The control for the different fan speeds is achieved by means of a resistive distributor protected against overheating by a special thermal fuse.

There is also a cursor on the control unit located under the knobs that adjusts the recirculation function mechanically by means of a flexible cable.

The control unit is lit with the side lights on.

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

The fan circuit is protected by a specific fuse and controlled by the passenter compartment air fan relay in the engine compartment junction unit.

The fan can only be switched on with the ignition ON.
